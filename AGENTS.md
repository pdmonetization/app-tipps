# App-Tipps editorial images

The site owner explicitly rejected the text-heavy workflow/diagram covers used
for the Gemini Notebook and CapCut clusters. These are a persistent visual
preference, not an optional suggestion.

- Never use slide-like flowcharts, numbered boxes, checklist panels, arrows,
  instructional diagrams, or SVG-to-WebP placeholders as article featured images
  or article-card thumbnails. Do not recreate that style in a different palette.
- Use original photographic editorial imagery or a well-composed illustration
  that suits the specific article. For generated raster art, use the built-in
  image-generation tool; do not substitute a programmatically drawn diagram.
- Give every new article its own distinct cover. Do not reuse one image across
  a cluster or make several barely different versions of the same composition.
- Keep headlines, labels, slogans and other large text out of the cover. The
  article title is already displayed below the thumbnail.
- Conceptual device scenes must not be presented as authentic app screenshots
  or evidence of hands-on testing. Describe them accurately in alt text.
- Inspect each image at thumbnail size before committing it. The subject should
  remain clear, the crop should work at 16:9, and any visible device interface
  should be plausible and incidental rather than invented product documentation.
- Optimize final covers as 1200 x 675 WebP images, preserve dimensions, and use
  the existing responsive-image pipeline. Update featuredImageAlt whenever the
  image changes. Remove obsolete rejected source graphics when unreferenced.

Diagrams may be used inside an article only when they explain something useful
and the owner explicitly requests or approves them. They are not cover art.
