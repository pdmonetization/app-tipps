The Impossible Quiz is one of the most fun and devious web games. It's been around since 2010, so it's not new to the scene, but the game remains as tricky and entertaining as ever! If you love challenging trivia and logic puzzles, this is the game!

Read on to learn more about The Impossible Quiz, how to play it online, and what makes it so unique. Then, let's test your patience with the game of the week. So, without further ado, let's hop in!

## **What Is The Impossible Quiz?**

While it may seem like a simple quiz at first, The Impossible Quiz will have you scratching your head in confusion. The game is no walk in the park, with bizarre questions and puzzles that make no sense. However, there are some answers to be found by those determined enough to find them. It is the most challenging trivia quiz available on the whole internet. Things get trickier when the person starts leveling up. Developers of the game want to test your patience with every passing question. So, do you have what it takes to play the game till the end?

## **Basics of the Impossible Quiz**

Initially named The Impossible Game, the Impossible Quiz was created by Spanish developer Senashe under the Slapp-Me-Do platform. The Flash game was first released in 2010 and became famous for its nearly impossible gameplay. The game consists of twenty riddles that players must answer correctly to advance. Each correct answer leads the player to the following riddle. Each incorrect answer forces the player to restart from scratch. Although the concept is simple, the difficulty level escalates quickly as the puzzles become more challenging.

## **The Game Is Challenging**

The Impossible Quiz challenges users with increasingly tricky riddles that will have you scratching your head in confusion and frustration. The questions are simple enough, but figuring out the answers requires a lot of thought, concentration, and sometimes even luck. With each correct answer, the player moves on to the next question until they reach the final riddle. At this point, they either win or lose, depending on their ability to solve it correctly.

Players worldwide are ready to swear that most questions are somewhat challenging. But wait until you get to the last ones that are easily considered impossible for all but a select few super-geniuses!

## **Gameplay**

![How to ipv](/images/2022/05/how-to-ipv.png)

The game consists of several mini-games that test the player's
knowledge. Depending on the level, the player is awarded different
amounts of points. The player earns up to three points for each correct
answer for the initial stages. However, the final level grants one point
regardless of how many questions are answered correctly.

If a player chooses not to answer, they will be forced to skip. No
progress could ever be made in the game until all questions are
responded to in their order of appearance. When choosing an incorrect
answer or if the player refuses to choose an answer when prompted, it is
shown whether they have got it right after a few seconds. The player
can also get a hint by clicking on the question mark (?) icon located in
the bottom left corner of the screen. This will show them the correct
answer but deducts one point from their score. The number of hints
available depends on the difficulty setting chosen by the player before
starting the quiz.

## **The Epic 10**

The final 10 questions of the quiz are called The Epic 10.
Unfortunately, the Epic 10 is not for the faint of heart. This section
of the game consists of ten questions that are always random and often
obscure. Fans of The Impossible Quiz wonder if there is a connection
between any or all of these final questions. Still, nothing has ever
been found. Although, it may be interesting to note that many earlier
questions are tied to later ones through various references and jokes.

## **Is the Impossible Quiz worth the time?**

The Impossible Quiz is worth playing if
you have time to kill and like solving mind-boggling riddles. On top of
that, the game plays a vital role in lightening up the mood and ensures
that you become relaxed after a tiring day at work.
