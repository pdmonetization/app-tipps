## **Introduction**

**Path of Exile** is a popular action role-playing game developed by **Grinding Gear Games**, and in recent years, it has expanded to mobile devices. This game guide aims to help players new to Path of Exile on mobile devices understand its mechanics, navigate its world, and progress through its various challenges.

## **Getting Started**

To start playing **Path of Exile** on mobile devices, players need to download and install the game from the **App Store** or **Google Play**. Once installed, players can create their character and choose their starting class. The game's user interface on mobile devices is different from the PC version, but it still offers plenty of customization options to make it comfortable to use.

Path of Exile features several unique gameplay mechanics, such as skill gems, passive skill tree, and currency items. These mechanics work the same way on mobile devices, but players may need to adjust their playstyle and strategy to fit the smaller screen. Understanding these mechanics is key to making progress in the game, and players can use these mechanics to create powerful builds and strategies.

Path of Exile mobile offers a complex and deep gameplay experience with various mechanics to master. Skill Gems provide players with new abilities, while the Passive Skill Tree offers a vast web of passive skills to invest in. Understanding Currency Items and crafting is crucial to improving equipment and making progress in the game. Items and equipment have unique properties, and choosing the right equipment for a particular build is essential. Different leagues offer varying challenges and rewards, and knowing the monsters and bosses' strengths and weaknesses is crucial to creating effective builds and strategies. Overall, mastering these gameplay mechanics is necessary to progress through Path of Exile mobile's challenges and create a build that suits the player's playstyle.

## **Classes and Builds**

Path of Exile features seven different classes, each with its unique playstyle and strengths. Understanding the strengths and weaknesses of each class is essential to creating effective builds. The game offers a massive number of build possibilities, and players can experiment with different combinations of skills, equipment, and passive points to create a build that suits their playstyle on mobile devices.

## **Quests and Storyline**

The game's main storyline follows the character's journey through the world of Wraeclast, a dark and dangerous land filled with monsters and other horrors. Completing quests and progressing through the story is essential to unlocking new areas and challenges in the game. There are also side quests that offer rewards and valuable experience points. On mobile devices, completing quests and progressing through the story can be challenging due to the smaller screen, but players can adjust the game's settings to make it more comfortable to play.

- **Main Storyline:** Path of Exile's main storyline revolves around a character called The Exile, who is banished to the forsaken continent of Wraeclast. The player must guide The Exile on a quest to seek vengeance against the corrupted rulers of Wraeclast and discover the truth behind their exile.
- **Acts:** The game is divided into ten Acts, with each act introducing new challenges and expanding the game's lore. Each act has a different storyline and quests that the player must complete to progress to the next act.
- **Side Quests:** In addition to the main storyline, there are also various side quests that players can undertake. These side quests often have unique rewards and can provide additional lore and backstory to the game's world.
- **NPC's:** Non-playable characters (NPCs) are scattered throughout the game world and offer quests and services to players. Some NPCs offer crafting services, while others provide quests or sell items.
- **Dialogue Choices:** Throughout the game, players will encounter various characters and have the option to make different dialogue choices. These choices can affect the outcome of quests and the player's relationship with NPCs.
- **Lore:** Path of Exile has a rich lore and backstory, which players can discover through various in-game sources. This lore provides context for the game's world and characters and adds depth to the overall gameplay experience.
