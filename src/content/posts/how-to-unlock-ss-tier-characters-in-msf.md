As of July 2022, Marvel Strike Force holds more than 150 unique characters on its roster. These Heroes can be obtained through campaign missions, the Blitz Store, the Arena Store, the Raid Store, the Alliance War Store, Supplies, Legendary Events, or by paying real money in one of the many Offers.

It is said that choosing your squad of heroes and villains is the most essential aspect of the entire gaming experience. They need you just as much as you need them. This is so that you can progress in the game and fight opponents with their aid.

Typically, a team comprises various heroes and villains, each with their own specialties. If you don't have heroes and villains in your collection, you won't be able to participate in any of the in-game events. In the guide below, we will look at the current top-tier Heroes and Villains in Marvel Strike Force and how you can unlock them.

## How do you get Ultron?

![Msf ultron](/images/2022/07/msf-ultron.png)

```
Class: Blaster
Origin: Tech
Allegiance: Villain
```

By successfully completing Dark Dimension's second stage, "Fear the Darkness," Ultron can be unlocked. Ultron will be unlocked with 5 stars after the first clearing and 7 stars after the second clearing.

As a player, you would need five Heroes outfitted in Tier 13 gear to participate and overcome "Fear the Darkness."

## How do you get Nick Fury?

![Msf nick fury](/images/2022/07/msf-nick-fury.png)

```
Class: Support
Origin: Skill
Allegiance: Hero
```

You will need five Kree Minions with five stars to unlock Nick Fury. The following is a list of Kree minions' farming locations:

1. **Kree Noble** – Premium Orb. Cosmic Conflicts 1-6
2. **Kree Royal Guard** – Premium Orb, Blitz Supplies, Blitz Orb
3. **Kree Oracle** – Premium Orb, Raid Supplies, Raid Orb
4. **Kree Cyborg** – Premium Orb, Beta Raid Orb, The Nexus 3-6, Cosmic Conflicts 2-3
5. **Kree Reaper** – Premium Orb, Heroes Assemble 5-6, Cosmic Conflicts 2-6

## How do you get Thanos?

![Msf thanos](/images/2022/07/msf-thanos.png)

```
Class: Protector  
Origin: Mystic  
Allegiance: Villain
```

Before Alpha, Beta, and Gama Raids advent, Thanos could only be unlocked through a unique Thanos Raid. However, after those raids, Thanos became accessible through Premium Orbs and Mega Orbs.

## How do you get Phoenix?

![Msf phoenix](/images/2022/07/msf-phoenix.png)

```
Class: Controller  
Origin: Mutant  
Allegiance: Hero
```

To unlock Phoenix, you need five heroes with Mystic, Controller, and Villain traits. Additionally, to unlock Phoenix, those heroes must have 6 stars.

The following is a list of the Heroes' best farming locations:

- **Ronan the Accuser** – Premium Orb, Legacy I Orb, Raid Supplies, Raid Orb
- **Loki** – Mega Orb, Premium Orb, Mystic Forces Rising 3-6
- **Hela** – Mega Orb, Premium Orb, Special Offers
- **Nobu** – Premium Orb, Hand Orb, Legacy I Orb, Villains United 6-3
- **Hand Assassin** – Premium Orb, Hand Orb, The Nexus 7-6
- **Mordo** – Mega Orb, Premium Orb, Legacy I Orb, Arena Store

## How do you get Black Bolt?

![Msf black bolt](/images/2022/07/msf-black-bolt.png)

```
Class: Blaster  
Origin: Bio  
Allegiance: Hero
```

You will need five Asgardian heroes with five stars to unlock Black Bolt. Heroes of Asgard can be farmed in:

- **Thor** – Legacy I Orb, Raid Supplies, Beta Raid Orb, Raid Orb,
- **Heimdall** – Mega Orb, Premium Orb, Arena Store
- **Hela** – Mega Orb, Premium Orb, Special Offers
- **Loki** – Mega Orb, Premium Orb, Mystic Forces Rising 3-6
- **Sif** – Currently unavailable except in Offers

## How do you get Ebony Maw?

![Msf ebony maw](/images/2022/07/msf-ebony-maw.png)

```
Class: Support  
Origin: Mystic  
Allegiance: Villain
```

You can unlock Ebony Maw by joining the Black & Ebony legendary event in MSF with a minimum of 5 Inhuman characters at 5 stars. Keep in mind that to unlock Ebony Maw first, you would have to unlock Black Bolt, and while doing so, you may focus on farming the other required Inhuman characters for the event through the following locations:

- **Quake** - Heroes 6-6 *(Campaign Energy Farm)* or Arena Store for 500 Arena Credits;
- **Crystal** - Arena Farm for 500 Arena Credits;
- **Karnak** - Nexus 8-6 *(Campaign Energy Farm)*;
- **Ms. Marvel** - Mystic 2-6 *(Campaign Energy Farm)*;
- **Yo-Yo** - Only available in Premium Orbs.

## How do you get Doctor Doom?

![Msf doctor doom](/images/2022/07/msf-doctor-doom.png)

```
Class: Controller  
Origin: Tech  
Allegiance: Villain
```

You must finish the Dark Dimension 4 in Marvel Strike Force to access Doctor Doom. It is regarded by many gamers as the most challenging mission thus far. You must be at Gear 15 to participate in the Dark Dimension 4.

You will receive a 5-star Doctor Doom, a 2-red star upgrade, and a variety of stuff when you finish the Dark Dimension 4. It's also said that if you want, you can utilize these goods to upgrade him to gear 15.
