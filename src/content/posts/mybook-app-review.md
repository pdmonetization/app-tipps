MyBook app is an excellent application for reading lovers who prefer to read from their digital devices rather than carry the book with them all the time. The app has thousands of readers from genres, from classic literature to modern-day bestsellers.

## What can you read on the MyBook app?

MyBook offers more than 200 000 books on its platform. Many books are available in Russian, so it is a wonderful app for you if you are a Russian speaker or are learning the language. Nevertheless, the app is not only for Russian speakers, as there are multiple books in English as well. Every month 2000 new books are added to the collection, and you can always find an interesting book to read next.

You can choose books from the new category, Top books, or browse the genres you love. The MyBook app has a wide selection of audiobooks too! So you can listen to the audio versions while traveling or simply whenever you do not feel like reading.

## MyBook app features

While using the MyBook app, you do not always have to be connected to the internet. The app allows readers to save books and continue reading offline!

The app has collections with recommendations. When you do not know what to read, you can check the collections and find the best books on the platform. When you start using the app, it will also give you personal recommendations based on your past readings. It is a great way to find books similar to the ones you love to read! The app also has rating features to help you choose the right book. You can check the quotes, notes, and ratings.

If you like to read from various devices, you do not have to worry about forgetting where you stopped reading. The app has full synchronization between different devices. So, for example, when you start reading on your smartphone, you can continue from your laptop from where you left it.

## MyBook app subscription plans

The MyBook app has two types of subscription plans - free and premium. The free subscription gives you access to a limited catalog of books. A Premium subscription comes with an opportunity to get one book per month from the rental catalog. In addition to that, this subscription comes with a free trial for the first 14 days of reading. You can check the premium subscription out and, in 14 days, decide if you like it more than the free one or not. You can always cancel the premium subscription plan, however, note that the subscription will automatically renew unless you cancel it.

## Enjoy reading and listening with the MyBook app

MyBook allows you to enjoy reading or listening to your favorite book. You can read any book you want, with or without an internet connection! Join thousands of readers today who enjoy the MyBook app. Download from Google Play or App Store and find the books you will fall in love with!
