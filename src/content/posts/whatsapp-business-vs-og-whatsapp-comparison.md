## **WhatsApp Business**

WhatsApp Business is a free app designed specifically for business owners. It allows businesses to interact with their customers in a professional and convenient manner. The app comes with a range of features, including quick replies, automated messages, and catalog integration. It also allows businesses to create a profile with their contact details, website, and business description.

The benefits of using WhatsApp Business for business owners are numerous. The app allows businesses to send and receive messages in a professional manner, which can help them build a better relationship with their customers. It also allows businesses to send automated messages and create custom labels for different conversations. Finally, the app comes with a range of analytics tools that can help businesses track their performance on the platform.

## **OG WhatsApp**

OG WhatsApp is a modified version of the original WhatsApp app that comes with additional features. The app is popular among people who want to customize their messaging experience. Some of the features of OG WhatsApp include the ability to hide your online status, customize the app's appearance, and send larger files.

The benefits of using OG WhatsApp for personal use are numerous. The app allows users to customize their messaging experience and control their privacy. It also allows users to send larger files, which can be useful for sharing media files like videos and images.

## **Comparison between WhatsApp Business and OG WhatsApp**

When it comes to comparing WhatsApp Business and OG WhatsApp, there are significant differences between the two apps. OG WhatsApp, on the other hand, comes with features that are designed to enhance the user's experience, such as the ability to customize the app's appearance and control their privacy.

In terms of pros and cons, WhatsApp Business is great for businesses that want to interact with their customers in a professional manner. It also comes with a range of analytics tools that can help businesses track their performance on the platform. However, the app may not be suitable for personal use. On the other hand, OG WhatsApp is great for people who want to customize their messaging experience and control their privacy. However, the app may not be suitable for businesses that want to interact with their customers in a professional manner.

- **Purpose and features**: WhatsApp Business is designed specifically for business owners, with features such as catalog integration, automated messages, and the ability to create a professional business profile. OG WhatsApp, on the other hand, is designed for personal use, with features such as the ability to customize the app's appearance, control your privacy, and send larger files.
- **Target audience**: WhatsApp Business is primarily targeted towards small and medium-sized businesses that want to interact with their customers in a professional manner. OG WhatsApp, on the other hand, is primarily targeted towards individuals who want more control over their messaging experience and privacy.
- **Analytics and tracking:** WhatsApp Business comes with a range of analytics tools that can help businesses track their performance on the platform. This includes data on message volumes, response times, and customer engagement. OG WhatsApp does not offer any analytics or tracking tools.
- **Security and privacy:** WhatsApp Business and OG WhatsApp both offer end-to-end encryption, which means that your messages are secure and can only be read by the sender and recipient. However, OG WhatsApp allows users to hide their online status and control their privacy settings in more detail than the original WhatsApp app or WhatsApp Business.
