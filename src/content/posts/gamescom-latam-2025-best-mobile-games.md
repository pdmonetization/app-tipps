The 2025 LATAM edition of the world's biggest gaming event, Gamescom, just came to an end on Sunday, May 4 in Sao Paolo, Brazil. The 5-day extravaganza showcased the best independent video games on the planet, defining the future of gaming!

*Take a peek at Gamescom LATAM 2025:*

  

The Best International Games (BIG) competition during Gamescom LATAM, known as '**BIG Festival**', nominated 100 titles across 18 different categories, including 'Best Mobile Game'.

There were 3 'Best Game' awards (global, Latin and Brazilian), winning not just a trophy, but a monetary prize too. The 'Popular Choice', picked by the audience, also won both. The other awarded categories featured 'Best New Game', 'Best VR/ XR', 'Best Multiplayer', 'Best Sound', 'Best Art', 'Best Narrative', 'Best Gameplay', 'Best Kids Game', 'Best Student Game' and 'Innovation'. There were also separate 'BIG Impact' awards across 3 categories: Education, Social Impact and 'Best Diversity Game'.

## Best Mobile Games 2025:

### **Level Devil**

[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.unept.leveldevil&hl=en) | [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/level-devil-not-a-troll-game/id6477413486)

![Level Devil game icon](/images/2025/05/LEVEL-DEVIL-GAME-APP-ICON-png.webp)
  

🥇 Level Devil is a US made mobile game and a **WINNER of the 'Best Mobile Game' award** during the BIG Festival at Gamescom LATAM 2025! It's a simple survival game with almost 200 levels, ideal for platformer lovers.

  

### **Mombo Combo Legacy**

[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.orube.mcl&hl=en) | [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/mombo-combo-legacy/id6615085388)

![Mombo Combo game icon](/images/2025/05/MOMBO-COMBO-GAME-APP-ICON-png.webp)
  

Another platformer game, taking you on a 'mombastic' monster-filled journey into unknown worlds... A great combo by the local Brazilian Orube Game Studio, who's also the developer of **Adventure Llama**.

  

### **PUNKO.IO**

[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Agonalea.Punko.io&hl=en) | [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/punko-io-roguelike-td/id6463813666)

![PUNKO.IO mobile game icon](/images/2025/05/PUNKO-IO-GAME-APP-ICON-png.webp)
  

A strategic, tower-defense game, perfect for players, who can't resist cute baddies' graphics!

### **Abalon**
