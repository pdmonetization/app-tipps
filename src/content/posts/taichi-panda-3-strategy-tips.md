Taichi Panda is a free mobile action/adventure RPG game created by Snail Games. Players journey through a visually enchanting world in a very three-quarter view perspective wherever they battle unique enemies and powerful bosses using accessible analog replicated controls. Taichi Panda 3: Dragon Hunter is an exquisite mobile MMORPG that blends components of eastern and Western fantasy. It reminds us of a mixture of World of Warcraft and Jade Empire.

## **Taichi Panda 3: Tips and Strategy**

**Assign Your Alliance to Random**

If you allow the game to assign your alliance randomly (you get the possibility to choose between Lion Empire and Panda Alliance), you'll earn a reasonably hefty gold and diamond bonus. If you'd like to have a bit of an advantage, in the beginning, tap the circle in the middle of the screen to be randomly sorted.

Note that you'll have different job classes open to you depending on which side you decide. Folks within the Lion Empire get to choose from berserk (melee), Priest (healer), Ice Mage (black mage), and Scout (ranger), whereas the Panda alliance gets access to the swordsman (melee), Healer, rune Mage (black mage), and Scout. Decide if you wish to possess the freedom to choose among these classes before going random, but it's worth noting that they're not drastically different.

**Don't Forget to Cash in On Your Free Rewards**

Taichi Panda 3 is generous, handing out daily log-in bonuses and alternative goodies for free only for playing the game. Tap the "bonus" button at the top right corner to see all of the free coins and other items you have got access to. This may update every few hours, and it's an excellent way to build up a substantial collection of gear early on.

**Tap On Your Active Quest to Auto-Run to the Following NPC or Objective**

This is a lifesaver, especially if you're playing on a phone that has a smaller screen. If you tap on your active quest on the left-hand side of the screen, your character will automatically start running to the NPC you wish to speak to. This may keep you from wasting time attempting to seek out NPCs amongst all of the player characters.

**Stagger Your Enemies**

When you fill the orange bar next to an enemy's health meter, you'll stagger them and exploit them temporarily helpless, whereas you unleash powerful combos on them. Fill up the stagger meter by attacking ruthlessly. Don't be afraid to use any spells at your disposal. Your MP regenerates quite quickly. You'll be able to conjointly hold down the attack button to automatically pitch into your opponent, making it much easier to fill the stagger meter.
