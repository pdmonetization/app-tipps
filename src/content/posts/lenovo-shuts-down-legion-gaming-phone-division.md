**Lenovo**, the Chinese multinational technology company, recently made a significant announcement that has sent shockwaves throughout the gaming industry. **It has been confirmed that Lenovo will be shutting down its Legion gaming phone division, thereby discontinuing the production of Legion gaming smartphones.**

## **The Rise of Legion Gaming Phone Division**

Lenovo's Legion gaming phone division was established in 2020, and its primary goal was to develop smartphones that catered to the needs of gamers. The division released two gaming smartphones, the **Legion Phone Duel** and the **Legion Phone Duel 2**, which were well-received by the gaming community.

Lenovo's Legion gaming phone division was established in 2020 as part of the company's expansion into the gaming market. The division was tasked with creating smartphones specifically designed for gamers, which would compete with established players like Asus, Razer, and Xiaomi.

The first product released by the Legion gaming phone division was the Legion Phone Duel, which launched in July 2020. The phone boasted impressive specs, including a Snapdragon 865+ processor, up to 16GB of RAM, and a 144Hz display. It also featured a unique design with a pop-up camera and side-mounted USB-C ports for easier charging during gameplay.

The Legion Phone Duel 2 was even more ambitious than its predecessor, with upgraded features such as a Snapdragon 888 processor, up to 18GB of RAM, and a 144Hz AMOLED display. It also featured a redesigned cooling system and improved dual trigger buttons for enhanced gameplay.

Overall, the Legion gaming phone division made a strong entry into the gaming smartphone market with its innovative products and focus on delivering high-quality gaming experiences to users. While the division may no longer be in operation, its legacy lives on, and the innovations it brought to the market will continue to inspire future developments in gaming phones.

## **Reason for Closure**

Despite the success of the Legion gaming phones, Lenovo has decided to shut down the division. The primary reason cited for the closure is the increasingly competitive nature of the gaming smartphone market, which made it difficult for Lenovo to maintain a profitable position.

The closure of Lenovo's Legion gaming phone division came as a surprise to many in the gaming community, as the division had only been established a year prior and had already released two successful gaming phones.

According to Lenovo, the decision to shut down the division was made as part of the company's broader restructuring efforts. Lenovo is looking to focus on its core businesses, which include personal computers and smart devices, and streamline its operations to improve efficiency and profitability.

The gaming phone market is still relatively small compared to the broader smartphone market, and competition is fierce. Established players like Asus and Razer have already made significant strides in the market, while other tech giants like Xiaomi and Samsung have also entered the space. With so many players vying for a slice of the market, it can be challenging for newer entrants to establish themselves and achieve profitability.

It's worth noting that Lenovo's decision to shut down the Legion gaming phone division does not mean that the company is pulling out of the gaming market entirely. Lenovo remains committed to its Legion brand, which produces gaming laptops, desktops, and accessories, and the company will continue to invest in these products. However, it does mean that Lenovo will no longer be directly competing in the gaming phone market.

## **Impact on Gaming Industry**

The closure of Lenovo's Legion gaming phone division has left many gamers disappointed, as the Legion phones were known for their high-quality features, such as powerful processors and advanced cooling systems. However, the closure is not expected to have a significant impact on the overall gaming industry, as there are other manufacturers that offer gaming smartphones.

## **Lenovo's Future Plans**

Lenovo has not provided any details about its future plans for the gaming market, but the company has confirmed that it will continue to offer other gaming products, such as laptops and desktops.

## **Reactions from Industry Experts**

Industry experts have expressed mixed reactions to the news of Lenovo's decision. Some have expressed disappointment, while others have stated that the closure was not entirely unexpected, given the challenges faced by the Legion gaming phone division.
