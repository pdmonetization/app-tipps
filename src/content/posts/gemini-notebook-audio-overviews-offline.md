Gemini Notebook Audio Overviews turn source material into spoken summaries. The practical attraction is reviewing a subject while walking or travelling. The practical limitation is that a fluent conversation can make an omitted condition or incorrect number easy to miss.

This researched guide was checked on October 6, 2026. It includes suggested prompts and a listening checklist, not a hands-on performance review. Start with our [first notebook guide](/gemini-notebook-mobile-first-notebook/) if you have not added sources yet.

## Create an overview with a clear purpose

Open a notebook containing relevant sources, then use Audio Overview in Studio. Google's [Audio Overview documentation](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16212820?hl=en) describes customization for format, language and topic focus, with some options limited by platform or language. You need edit access to generate or delete audio. Generation can take a few minutes.

Instead of requesting a discussion of everything, choose the question you want to understand. For a report about two train routes, an original suggested prompt is: “Explain the trade-offs between the routes for a weekday commuter. Separate travel time, changes and cost. Say when the sources do not answer a question.”

Do not give the app a timetable from last year and expect a current travel recommendation. The quality of the source matters before the style of the audio does.

## Download for offline listening

Google's [mobile instructions](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16296687?co=GENIE.Platform%3DiOS&hl=en) describe background playback and a Download control for Audio Overviews. Downloaded audio appears in the app's Downloaded section. Offline audio playback does not mean that source import, new generation or chat works offline.

Before a journey, finish generation while connected, download the correct overview and briefly test playback without a network connection. That test is a recommended check for your device, not something we have performed for this article.

## Use a pre-trip checklist

| Check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Notebook title matches the subject | Avoid listening to an older project |
| Sources have appropriate dates | A polished summary can still be based on stale information |
| Download has completed | Starting a stream is different from storing audio |
| Audio plays with the network disconnected | Confirms the file is available on your device |
| Headphones and playback volume work | Avoid discovering a simple setup problem on the train |

For study material, write down two questions before listening. Afterwards, answer them without replaying the entire overview. If you cannot explain the point yourself, listen to the relevant section or return to the reading.

## Check facts after listening

Suppose a practice source says: “Route A takes 40 minutes and requires one change. Route B takes 55 minutes and is direct. Weekend times differ.” An overview that calls A universally faster has lost the weekday/weekend qualification.

Ask a follow-up question about that condition and inspect the source. Keep a small checklist of exact figures, dates and exceptions to verify. Audio is useful for orientation; the original passage is better for checking precise wording.

## If audio will not download or play

Check the network during generation, available device storage and whether you are signed into the notebook's account. If the overview is missing, confirm that generation completed and that you opened the correct notebook. Google's audio documentation notes that some Play Books publisher restrictions can prevent artifact downloads; do not assume every notebook can be exported.

Avoid deleting the notebook as an early troubleshooting step. Record what failed first. For source failures, follow our [import guide](/gemini-notebook-source-import-problems/). For active revision afterwards, use [flashcards and quizzes](/gemini-notebook-flashcards-quizzes/).
