This MSF Dark Dimension guide follows a mode that has grown from one late-game challenge into a nine-stage progression path through the Marvel Strike Force roster. Each stage asks for a new gear, Iso-8 or trait investment and usually awards a character that helps with later endgame content.

This Marvel Strike Force Dark Dimension guide replaces our outdated DD1–DD5 walkthrough with the current DD1–DD9 structure. It is designed for planning: see the broad requirement and reward first, then use the dedicated guide for the stage you are actually building.

The game has reworked older Dark Dimensions and their rewards over time. Always confirm an irreversible promotion against the live mission-selection screen and Scopely's planner.

## How Dark Dimension progression works

Dark Dimension nodes preserve progress between attempts. A team does not need to clear every wave in one battle; damage remains, and eligible characters can return after the mode's recovery rules allow it.

First and timed runs normally contain the meaningful character, gear and promotion rewards. Replaying a completed stage after those reward runs may still be possible, but it is not the same progression value.

The central planning problem is permanent investment. Gear equipped to the wrong character cannot simply be moved to the better choice. Every Dark Dimension plan should therefore balance three goals:

1. Clear the required nodes with a functional team.
2. Spread scarce origin and unique gear across the roster.
3. Invest in characters that remain useful in Arena, Raids, War or Cosmic Crucible.

## DD1 and DD2: build the first endgame core

DD1 is the first roster check and now leads to Kestrel rewards. DD2 raises the requirement to Gear Tier 13 and awards Ultron across its reward runs.

These stages are old enough that modern characters can overpower encounters that once required very specific combinations. Do not search for a preserved 2019 lineup and build obsolete characters solely because an old guide called them essential. Use the strongest eligible five already supporting current campaign, Raid and Arena progress.

Sustain is still valuable. Healing, cleanse, ability control and percentage-based effects can reduce the number of attacks needed, but cross-mode usefulness should break close decisions.

## DD3 and DD4: start planning by trait

DD3 introduces Gear Tier 14 and restricted trait sections. DD4 follows at Gear Tier 15 and awards Doctor Doom.

This is where one universal team stops being enough. Build separate City, Global and Cosmic groups, then prepare the required Legendary section. The exact node structure differs by stage, so use the in-game filter before equipping gear.

Do not spread resources evenly across 20 characters on day one. Finish the first restricted section, then equip the next group in mission order. Gear earned inside the run can shorten the following build.

## DD5: the Gear Tier 16 Dormammu run

DD5 uses Gear Tier 16 and continues the trait-section model. Its first and timed completions build Dormammu from the initial unlock toward the higher-star reward.

The best DD5 team in 2026 is not the same as the best team at launch. Modern kits and higher base statistics make many current characters safer investments. Prefer eligible characters that control waves, survive repeated attacks and already belong to a relevant team elsewhere.

Players returning after a long break should resist finishing an old half-built recommendation automatically. Compare the remaining gear cost with a newer alternative before promoting either one.

## DD6: prepare Gear Tier 18 for Super Skrull

DD6 moves to Gear Tier 18 and awards Super Skrull. Its sections include unrestricted opening missions followed by Global, Cosmic, City and specialist Legendary requirements.

The key planning lesson is to count gear pieces, not just character names. Two teams can use the same number of Bio characters while requiring very different quantities of the scarce GT18 pieces. A cheaper eligible character with excellent wider utility may accelerate the run more than a perfect Dark Dimension specialist.

## DD7: Gear Tier 19 and the Mephisto unlock

DD7 requires Gear Tier 19 and awards Mephisto. City, Global and Cosmic restrictions exclude some premium classes before Legendary and Mythic characters receive their own sections.

Read every exclusion literally. A character can possess the correct location trait and still be blocked because the mission excludes Legendary or Mythic characters. Check the live selection screen rather than relying on memory from DD6.

Mephisto becomes available for the timed run after the first completion, which can materially change the second clear. Save notes on difficult waves and cooldown timing from the first attempt so the stronger roster converts into a faster run.

## DD8: Purple Iso-8 and Odin

Dark Dimension VIII replaced the usual universal gear gate with Purple Iso-8 Level 3. Scopely still recommended strong gear, but [the official DD8 rules](https://marvelstrikeforce.com/en/updates/dark-dimension-8) made T3 Level 3 Iso-8 the formal character requirement.

Its restricted groups are:

- City Hero, excluding Legendary
- City Villain, excluding Legendary
- Global Hero, excluding Legendary
- Global Villain, excluding Legendary
- Cosmic or Legendary

The first run awards a 5-Yellow-Star Odin and 2 Red Stars. The timed run takes him to 7 Yellow Stars and 4 Red Stars, alongside additional Iso-8, gear and other resources.

DD8 is a warning against assuming every new Dark Dimension simply adds one gear tier. Read the actual gate before farming; the expensive resource can change from gear to Iso-8.

## DD9: Gear Tier 21 and Annihilus

Dark Dimension IX opened on April 15, 2026. Every mission requires five Gear Tier 21 characters, and the full run uses about 25 characters across five restricted groups.

The sections are City, Cosmic and Global excluding Mythic, Epic and Legendary characters; Legendary or Villain Epic; and Mythic or Hero Epic. Unrestricted missions appear at the beginning and end.

The first completion unlocks Annihilus at 5 Yellow Stars and 2 Red Stars. The timed run takes him to 7 Yellow Stars and 4 Red Stars. His remaining Red Stars come from the separate Annihilus Saga Epilogue.

## How to choose characters for any Dark Dimension

### Prioritize control and sustain

Dark Dimension is a sequence of long PvE waves. Healing, revive control, cleanse, ability block, stun, turn manipulation and effects that scale against large health pools are often more valuable than a short opening burst.

### Build for more than one mode

A Raid character used every day usually creates more account value than a DD-only specialist. When two candidates can clear the section, choose the one that advances another current team.

### Spread origins and uniques

Record Bio, Tech, Skill, Mystic and Mutant costs before equipping. Then check unique pieces separately. A seemingly balanced five-origin roster can still contain a shared unique bottleneck.

### Finish sections in order

Equipping one character for every future section produces no usable team. Complete the next eligible five, enter the nodes and use the rewards to help build what follows.

### Keep later slots flexible

Dark Dimension farms can take months. New characters can arrive before the final section is reached. Reserve resources for likely choices, but delay marginal later promotions until the earlier team is nearly complete.

## Dark Dimension mistakes that waste the most gear

- Building a historical launch team without checking the current roster
- Ignoring Legendary, Mythic or Epic exclusions
- Equipping a character before counting shared uniques
- Choosing five characters from the same origin without enough inventory
- Treating the unrestricted opening as a reason to build another five
- Spending heavily for a first clear when the mode is permanently available
- Forgetting that the timed run rewards a stronger version of the unlock character

## Where should a new or returning player start?

Start with the earliest unfinished stage that awards a character or resources your account still needs. Do not skip a manageable older Dark Dimension merely because DD9 is the current headline; Kestrel, Ultron, Doctor Doom, Dormammu, Super Skrull, Mephisto and Odin form part of the progression that can support later content.

If the account is years away from GT21, the practical next step is not a DD9 shopping list. It is a focused five for the next available stage, an active Alliance and disciplined daily resource use.
