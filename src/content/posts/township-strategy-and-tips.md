Township is a strategy and management that lets you build a farm, a city, and all the infrastructure that surrounds them. You will start off with a small plot where you can plant some crops and build a few houses, but as you buy new lots and the size of your city grows, you will be able to add many more buildings to your Township.

The gameplay is straightforward, as almost every action is carried out by dragging objects from one place to another. For example, you need to touch the screen to plant seeds and pass a finger over the screen to harvest your crops.

## **Township: Game Tips and Tricks**

1. **Relax and Build a Free Town**

Township is an open-world game which means that you do not have any missions or quests to complete. You only need to use your creativity to choose buildings & establishments and build your city. However, you need to create a vibrant, thriving metropolis to win the game’s premium currency, experience points, and coins.

![Township ipv 1](/images/2023/02/township-ipv-1.jpg)

2. **Complete More Orders to Earn More Coins**

In Township, completing orders is the most reliable way to earn coins. Click on the Helicopter buttons to access orders and complete them or signal people to submit orders to you. Some orders are not worth it, so you need to examine orders to choose the ones offering great deals.

Orders that are not chosen can bring a lot of confusion in the gameplay, so you need to do away with them. Hint: Click on the trash bin to get rid of useless orders.

3. **Concentrate More On Farming**

The reason why you need to focus more on farming is that your city grows and thrives mainly on crops. So to build a bustling and active city, you need to plant a lot of organic produce. The game allows you to unlock crops and produce the ones that you think will benefit your city the most.

![Township ipv 3](/images/2023/02/township-ipv-3.jpg)

It is recommended to grow more wheat as this crop is in high demand and can bring more profits. So always select a more extensive plot for wheat and buy a good number of inputs for the crop. As you plant and harvest crops, keep an eye on the orders in the queue to ensure you supply the products accordingly.

4. **Get The Right Construction Materials**

Construction materials in Township come in random, and you should look out for them to pick the ones that match your construction work. The materials come with trains, and the game enables you to send trains to go with materials that match your city. Getting suitable materials can be complex, but you can still send trains until you get the best materials.

5. **Expand Your City Early In the Game**

Developing your city in the initial levels of the game is much cheaper than doing it in the middle or last stages. You will find that there is a lot of space, a good supply of materials & decorations, and reasonable access to other components in the initial stages of the game to help you build your city faster.

![Township ipv 5](/images/2023/02/township-ipv-5.jpg)

6. **Make Achievements and Win Valuables**

When you construct a good portion of the game and move from one level to another, you gain significant valuables and a lot of bonuses.

The coins can get you Decorations, Factories, and Community Buildings. The XPS can enable you to level up and help you lock more Factories, Decorations, Crops, and Community Buildings. The T-cash can do almost everything, but it is not easy to earn it.

7. **Interact with Friends On Social Networks**

The game enables you to interact with other players on Facebook. Communicating with friends can help you develop your city faster as you can use friends’ help. You can also exchange trains and planes with your friends for things that you are running short of at certain moments.
