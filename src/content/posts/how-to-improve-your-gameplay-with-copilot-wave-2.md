**Ever thought Artificial Intelligence could be your best gaming buddy?** Well, after the second wave of innovations to Microsoft Copilot, powered by GPT-4o, it can now play alongside you, giving you real-time advice and recommendations!

## Microsoft Copilot AI as a gaming assistant

The advanced AI features of Copilot Wave 2 allow the application to learn a game inside out, so it can guide you through it, while you're actually playing it. You can also ask it specific questions or request tips and tricks on improving your performance. And thanks to the ChatGPT Omnimodel integration, you can do all that in a natural language, without having to rephrase or repeat yourself.

## How to get real-time gameplay assistance from Copilot

If you have **Copilot Pro**, all you need to do is open the game and share your screen with Copilot!
