## Key Features:

Loopsie prides itself on user-friendliness. You don't need to be a tech wizard to navigate the app. With its intuitive interface, you can start creating stunning 3D photos with just a few taps.

### 2. Cinemagraphs Made Simple

Cinemagraphs are living photos that blend static elements with subtle, repeated motions. Loopsie simplifies the cinemagraph creation process, allowing you to select the areas you want to animate effortlessly.

### 3. AI-Powered Stabilization

Say goodbye to shaky videos. Loopsie's AI stabilization feature ensures that your animations are smooth and professional-looking, even if your original footage wasn't.

### 4. Full Control

While Loopsie makes the creation process easy, it doesn't skimp on control. You can fine-tune your animations, adjust speed, and experiment with various effects to achieve your desired look.

### 5. Social Sharing

Once your masterpiece is ready, share it with the world! Loopsie lets you export your creations in multiple formats, making it a breeze to showcase your 3D photos on your favorite social media platforms.
