Are you tired of shelling out big bucks for your monthly phone bill? Wish you could chat and call your friends without worrying about those extra charges? Look no further because the TextNow app is here to revolutionize the way you stay connected. ***In this review, we'll dive deep into the world of TextNow, the app that offers unlimited calls and texts - all without breaking the bank.***

## **What's TextNow, Anyway?**

TextNow is a game-changer for mobile communication. *It's not just an app*; it's your all-in-one solution for calling, texting, and even video calls. Whether you're an Android enthusiast or an iOS lover, TextNow is available for both platforms, so no one gets left out.

## **Unlimited Everything!**

The highlight of TextNow is its unlimited offerings. With the app, you get unlimited calling and texting within the United States and Canada. That's right, unlimited! No more worrying about those overage charges or running out of texts during a heated conversation.

## **Keep Your Number or Get a New One**

TextNow offers you the flexibility to choose. You can either keep your current phone number or get a brand-new one. Plus, you can even choose the area code you prefer, which is a cool feature if you want to keep things local or if you're relocating.

![Textnow](/images/2023/11/TextNow.png)

## **All the Bells and Whistles**

TextNow isn't just about basic communication; it comes packed with features that make your experience truly awesome. Here are some highlights:

**1. Voicemail Transcription:** Say goodbye to listening to long voicemails. TextNow transcribes them for you so you can read them at your own pace.

**2. Customizable Wallpapers:** Make your TextNow experience your own by changing the app's wallpaper to something that suits your style.

**3. Call Forwarding:** Busy? No problem. You can forward your TextNow calls to another number when you can't answer.

**4. Group Texting:** Start group chats with friends and family to keep everyone in the loop.

**5. Stickers and Emojis:** Express yourself better with a wide range of stickers and emojis.

## **Ads? Yes, but...**

TextNow is free, and it's gotta pay the bills somehow, right? That's why you'll see some ads within the app. They're not too intrusive, but if you prefer an ad-free experience, you can opt for a premium subscription.

## **Premium Perks**

If you decide to go premium, TextNow offers even more perks. You'll enjoy an ad-free experience, faster customer support, and the ability to use TextNow on multiple devices. It's worth considering if you love the app and want to take your experience to the next level.

## **Final Thoughts**

TextNow is a game-changer for anyone looking to cut down on their phone bill without sacrificing communication quality. With unlimited calls and texts, customization options, and the choice of keeping or getting a new number, it's a versatile app that's hard to pass up.

Give TextNow a try and see how it can transform the way you connect with friends and family. It's the future of affordable and convenient communication in the palm of your hand. Say goodbye to overpriced phone plans and hello to TextNow – where calls and texts are truly unlimited!
