## Quick verdict

AliExpress can offer unusually broad selection and low prices, but it is a marketplace rather than one retailer. The seller, listing, delivery method and local import rules matter more than the app’s polished checkout.

## What it offers in 2026

The app lets shoppers compare listings from many merchants, follow shipments, message sellers and open disputes. “Choice” listings may offer platform-managed logistics or service promises in eligible markets, but the exact guarantee must be read on each product page.

## Main features

- Large international marketplace catalogue
- Seller ratings, order counts and customer photos
- Choice and consolidated delivery on eligible products
- Order tracking and in-app disputes
- Coupons and app promotions with conditions

## Pricing and fees

The displayed item price may exclude VAT, customs, local handling or optional shipping depending on destination. Promotions can require minimum spend or selected payment methods. Compare the final checkout total and realistic delivery date.

## Limitations and risks

Avoid counterfeit-branded products, implausible specifications and sellers that ask for off-platform payment. Read recent reviews for the exact variant, record the unboxing of high-value items and open disputes within the stated window.

## Who is it best for?

Best for inexpensive, non-urgent, easy-to-evaluate products. Use an authorised local seller for safety-critical items, warranties or purchases where fast returns matter.

## Our practical recommendation

Check the live checkout, permissions and cancellation terms on your own device. Use the free tier, trial or shortest plan first where possible, and keep transaction records for paid services.
