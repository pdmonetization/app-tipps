Dawn of Titans is a freemium action strategy game for Android and iOS. You are the newly appointed ruler of a beautiful kingdom in the sky. There are other lords who are not playing nicely, so it is up to you to lead your armies into battle. Among your allies are the titular Titans, massive heroes with the strength of multiple armies.

The game has amazing graphics. The effects and textures in this game are gorgeous. The environment design is also awesome. Your flying fortress is a spectacle in itself, featuring beautiful mountains and lush forests.

## **Dawn of Titans: Game Tips and Secrets**

- **Collect Mighty Titans**

Whether they are protecting your Kingdom or marching at the head of your army, there is nothing more dangerous than a Titan. Titans come in many different sizes and shapes. Build an army that supports your Titans’ strengths, and you will annihilate your opponents.

![Dawn of titans ipv 1](/images/2023/02/Dawn-of-Titans-ipv-1.jpg)

- **Upgrade Your Castle**

The most important building in your City is a castle. When upgrading your Castle, you unlock new buildings, which in turn can produce and upgrade new spells and units. If you want the biggest City and the strongest army, the Castle is where you should focus your attention.

- **Battlefield Tips**

Be confident and play the battlefield to your advantage. Whether you choose to fight the fire-throwing Unak Grenadiers with the blistering speed of the Mossmane Panthers, counter the giant club-wielding Ragnar with the deadly Elithen Pikemen, or protect your skillful Archers with defensive units of Spearmen. Understanding the strengths and weaknesses of your units in battle is the key to claiming victory.

![Dawn of titans ipv 3](/images/2023/02/Dawn-of-Titans-ipv-3.jpg)

- **Complete the Campaign or Fight Against Players**

Choose your path to forge a powerful Kingdom. The battle against other players or play through the single-player campaign to defeat the evil Almarand, an evil Titan who seeks ultimate power and will stop at nothing to get it.

- **Raid and Capture Lands to be the Best**

You will earn Gold, Food, and Victory point when you raid an enemy player’s Land, and when you capture a Land will add it to your own Kingdom. Captured Lands generate resources over time, so use these extra resources to upgrade your Kingdom and become more powerful.

![Dawn of titans ipv 4](/images/2023/02/Dawn-of-Titans-ipv-4.jpg)

- **Join an Alliance with Your Friends**

Make an alliance with friends to conquer the world around you. Reach Castle Level Three to create or join an Alliance, then battle other players to earn Victory Points (VP). The Alliance with the most Victory Points in a League earns fantastic rewards, including Titans

- **Control Your Units**

Basic controls and advanced controls are both present in this game. For basic controls, tap a unit to select it, and then tap the enemy. This will guide your units automatically to your enemy. For advanced controls, tap a unit to select it, and then create your movement path by dragging the unit’s shield to the enemy. Advanced controls are great for flanking maneuvers and for catching the enemy by surprise.

- **Defend Your Land**

You must protect your Lands against the enemy. Do not allow players to steal your hard-earned rewards. Instead, preserve your power by assigning Titans and troops to Lands you have captured. This will make it difficult for players to raid or capture your valuable Gold, Food, and Victory Points.

- **Relics and Skills**

Relics are game-changing items that can be equipped with your Titan or buildings in your City. Relics equipped to buildings increase productivity, while those equipped to Titans improve the attributes of your Titans and troops or can grant additional elemental damage to your Titan’s attacks. Every Titan has one or more Skills that also improve the health, armor, or attack power of the Titan itself or surrounding units. Combine the correct Relics with your Titan’s Skills to create a devastatingly strong Titan who can emerge victorious over any opponent.

![Dawn of titans ipv 2](/images/2023/02/Dawn-of-Titans-ipv-2.jpg)

- **Play Unique Events**

The ever-changing world of the game means that a new event is always right around the corner. Play unique event campaigns expanding the lore of *Dawn of Titans* and earning powerful new Titans, which change the way you play the game.
