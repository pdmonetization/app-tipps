- **Save time** by activating a mobile plan in seconds (calls/ texts or Internet only)

- **No roaming charges** or any other extra costs when travelling abroad (regardless of location)
- **Full control** over your mobile phone bill
- **Be ECO-friendly** by using a virtual card, instead of a piece of plastic
