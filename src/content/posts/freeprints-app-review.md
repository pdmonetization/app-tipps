In today's mobile world, we keep photos on our smartphones and tablets. We save hundreds of selfies and pictures and sometimes never return them. But some photos memorize the most special moments, and you do not want to put them on your Instagram and Facebook wall only.

Instead, you want to hang them in your room or put them at your working desk. The FreePrints app will help you do that without the hassle and for free!

## What is FreePrints?

Printing photos sounds like an easy task. However, it is not that simple. You need to find a good photo kiosk, send pictures via email, social media, or transfer via the USB, pay for printing, and in the end, you might not be happy with the results. If it sounds like you, you are not alone. There are plenty of people with the same issue.

The FreePrints app can save you from all that trouble. The app belongs to the Texas-based photo lab and offers up to 85 prints a month for free. Using FreePrints is easy; choose photos, pay for shipping, and receive prints! You can select the pictures from your phone or directly from social media and order 4x6 prints. You will receive printed photos in several days at your mailbox – the quality of the images is guaranteed! Printing pictures is free, although you will have to pay a small shipping fee.

## How does the FreePrints app work?

To print photos through the FreePrints app, you should select photos from your Camera Roll, Facebook, Instagram, Dropbox, Google Photos, Microsoft Drive, Amazon Cloud Drive, or Flickr. Once you have your images selected, choose the quantity and order your prints. They will be delivered to your doorstep!

With the FreePrints app, you can order up to 85 prints per month, which makes up to 1000 photos a year! You are only limited in the size of your prints, as FreePrints offers only standard, 4x6 sized prints for free. You can be sure about the quality; FreePrints uses deluxe glossy and premium matte photo papers from Kodak and FujiFilm only.

## Is the FreePrints app free?

The FreePrints app is free. You can download it from Google Play or the App Store. There is no subscription or any kind of commitment required. You can keep the app on your phone and use it when you want! You will get up to 85 4x6 prints per month for free, which makes up to 1000 prints a year! However, there is a limit of one print per photo.

You will have to pay for the shipping fee, which is a minimum of $1.99 but never goes above $9.99. Does not matter how big your shipment is; you can be sure that you will not have to pay more than $9.99 for shipping. It is much less than what you might have to pay for printing 85 photos anywhere else!

## What else can you get with the FreePrints app?

Maybe you want more than 85 standard-sized prints - no problem. The app allows you to order extra prints for an additional fee. You will be charged for the additional photos if you order more than 85 standard-sized photos.

The app offers large prints as well. You can order 5x5, 5x7, 8x10, 11x14, 12x18, 16x20 and 20x30 size prints. Simply add the sizes you want in your app, checkout, and wait for the high-quality pictures to be delivered to you!

## Is the FreePrints app safe to use?

Privacy is paramount when you are sending your photos to someone. With the FreePrints app, you can be sure that all your photos are kept private and safe. The pictures you transmit via the app are stored safely; only you can access them once they are printed. The FreePrints app uses the latest and best-of-breed security solutions to ensure the protection of your photos. If you want to learn more about it, you can visit the app's Privacy Policy.
