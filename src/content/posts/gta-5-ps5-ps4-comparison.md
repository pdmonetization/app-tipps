Grand Theft Auto V for the PlayStation 5 console got officially released on March 15.

That new-gen console gave Rockstar Games the chance to continue its'
spree of unprecedented longevity. It might have been ancient history if
it was any other game, but Grand Theft Auto marks 9 years of existence
this year. Despite the excellent name, gaming fans of the sequence can't
help but wonder if the game is any different on the next-gen console.
One of the top promises that Rockstar Games vowed to meet is improved
loading times and graphics enhancement. For this, the gaming studio
shared details still before the release that the game will hold three
graphic modes:

1. Fidelity Mode
2. Performance Mode
3. Performance Mode with Ray Tracing

[Video Games Chronicle](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/features/gallery-heres-how-gta-5-compares-on-ps5-vs-ps4/)
made an advanced move and compared the game on both PS consoles with
the game running at native 4K, and ray tracing enabled to show off the
PS5 version's graphical power. After reviewing the beautiful graphics,
we hate to say it, but the new version makes the PS4 game look quite
old. We are talking ***old,*** as in ***one-foot-in-the-grave-old***.

Still, check if you can notice any distinguishable difference in the sharpness or the colors in the compared imagery.
