Slickdeals is one of the largest deal-sharing communities that offer users the rating, sharing as well as reviews of the deals and coupons for fans. People use it on a regular basis to search, rate, and find the best deals from their favorite shops. Slickdeals front page prompts up the running as well as upcoming deals for the users; for example, if Michael Kores has 30% off, Walmart has some discounted offers or others.

## **What is this app all about?**

Slickdeals are known as a deal-sharing platform that enables members to find deals around the world and share them with the community. It allows users to showcase deals or advertise over the site within the mentioned categories. The community can vote and comment, and the most famous deals will be moved up to the Popular Deals forum.

## **How does the Slickdeals app function?**

The community members using this app share the deal over the forums. Each deal is vetted by its community members, and the one who gets most of the votes goes to the top of the list of popular deals. Snagging a perfect deal can be done only if you reach the popular deal forum at the right time before the masses reach it.
The deal editor screens down the popular deals once it gets enough votes to promote them on the front page.

## **Why would you choose Slickdeals?**

It allows you to browse your favorite deal or coupon according to its category. For example, if you are interested in shopping for new clothes, you can browse that in the search bar and have a look at the discounted coupons and deals.

### Pros

It is a very useful app for customers who love shopping at discounted prices with promotional codes, vouchers, and deals running on the websites. It is a user-friendly app that works well on smartphones, laptops, or PC.

## Cons

The cons of this app are few as there are very few user reviews regarding this app. Some people might face issues in order to downloading the deals and face app working issues.
**Awesome features of the Slickdeals app:**

- Custom forums
- Deal Alerts
- Discount codes
- Deal highlights
- Community votes
- Mobile usage
- Curated FrontPage
- Crowdsourced details

Deal Alerts
To get prompt notifications regarding the upcoming new deals, you just need to set up the deal alert and get the best coupons and deals for the categories you have been looking for. You can also choose slick deals once you have filled up the cart and are planning to check out. You have a last-minute chance to check the coupons on its front page and save money on that specific store. The editors of this app are quite active as they immediately remove the expired deals and coupons from the web page to avoid confusion.
