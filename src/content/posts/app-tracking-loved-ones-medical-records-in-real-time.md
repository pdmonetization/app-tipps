Long-term care homes can now provide 24/7 access to the medical files of their patient residents through a simple app! The software program [EngagePlus](https://www.engageplus.io/) allows relatives to get instant health notifications about their loved ones' condition, medications and even staff notes.

This convenient way of providing patient information is not only extremely time saving, but most importantly, it gives invaluable piece of mind to family members...

The director of **'Isabel and Arthur Meighen Manor' care home in Toronto**, which was the first non-profit center to implement the app in Canada, admits that even though the innovation was daunting at first, the benefits are unparalleled.

*Learn more on CTV News:*

  

EngagePlus is a Canadian-developed management platform, originally created for the education business. However, the software's automation and notification capabilities seem to have found an invaluable application in the way healthcare establishments can save their often scare resources!
