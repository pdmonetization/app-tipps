If you care about the environment, you can do your bit for the planet even when you're on the move! And the little green friends, that can help you with that, are just a download away from you :)

Here are some clever eco-friendly apps, you can use to reduce **your carbon footprint**, lessen **food waste**, encourage **recycling** and support **companies, helping the environment**.

---

## Apps that reduce CARBON EMISSIONS

[BlaBlaCar](https://www.blablacar.co.uk/) is a global travel app, focused on ride sharing. And less vehicle emissions mean a much healthier environment! **Download the BlaBlaCar app for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.comuto&pcampaignid=pcampaignidMKT-Other-global-all-co-prtnr-py-PartBadge-Mar2515-1) or [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/blablacar-carpooling-and-bus/id341329033?ign-itsct=apps_box_badge&ign-itscg=30200)**.

[Zipcar](https://www.zipcar.com/) - the green car rental and sharing app, committed to eliminating the need for owning a vehicle. Zipcar make this possible by providing swift and hassle-free car rental on the go 24/7! **Get the Zipcar app on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zc.android&hl=en) or [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zipcar-cars-on-demand/id329384702)**.

---

## Apps that diminish FOOD LOSS & WASTE

[Too Good To Go](https://www.toogoodtogo.com/) is a brilliant app, connecting shops and restaurants that have excess food with local people, so good food doesn't get thrown in the bin! **Find if there is one near you on "Too Good To Go" for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app.tgtg) or [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/dk/app/too-good-to-go-end-food-waste/id1060683933)**.

[Olio](https://olioapp.com/en/) is another great app, dedicated to ending food waste, as well as clothes, books, toys and other redundant items that can be reused. **Check out Olio on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.olioex.android) or [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/olio/id1008237086)**.

---

## THE ULTIMATE GREEN APP

👑 **[Ecosia](https://www.ecosia.org/) 👑** is the greenest mobile application, search engine and browser! **So far, the company has planted over 200 million trees all across the world**. You too can be part of this by simply downloading the app and using the [Ecosia search engine](https://www.ecosia.org/) and [Ecosia browser](https://www.ecosia.org/browser/confirmation). It's not only 100% free, but 100% of the profit it generates (from advertising) goes directly into saving the planet! And the more you use Ecosia, the more money goes to the environment. So, **do give Ecosia a go on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ecosia.android&hl=en) or [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ecosia/id670881887)**.

You can discover more green companies and join ecological communities on **[NatureHub.com](http://naturehub.com) or through the Nature Hub app on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/naturehub/id1439011467)**.
