Whether you're a frequent traveler or you're dreaming about your next holiday, there is an app that literally generates **the best hotel deals of the best booking engines**.

**[Hotellook](https://hotellook.tp.st/1RAOGmfX)** is a price comparison platform, partnering with ***Booking.com**, **Expedia**, **Hotels.com**, **Agoda*** and the rest 60+ industry leaders. And downloading their mobile application makes finding the best accommodation deals even easier. Furthermore, to ensure you get an unbeatable rate, they now work with small family-run hotels too, often not listed by large booking engines.

Currently, the total number of hotels, apartments, hostels and guest houses you can find on **[Hotellook](https://hotellook.tp.st/1RAOGmfX)**, **exceed 1 million!**

The Hotellook app, available on both Google Play and the Apple App Store, is free to download and **absolutely free** to book hotel stays. It operates with final room pricing without any hidden fees or extra costs. They earn a commission from their travel partners so they can offer you the best possible price on your accommodation.

You can sort results not only by price, but by star and guest **ratings** too. And if you’re after a specific area or attraction in your chosen destination, you can easily select it on the **Hotellook map** to find the nearest lodging options. Or just search for hotels near your current location.

Last but not least, to grab an even better deal, you can subscribe for **price alerts** and get instant app notifications! Like if you have your eye on a specific holiday spot, you can monitor how price changes over time within the Hotellook app.
