Make the most of summer 2025 with a big smile and 10 of the brightest mobile apps:

## 🌅 Summer Weather Apps

- [**Weawow**](https://weawow.com/): Follow the weather forecast this summer and enjoy stunning seasonal photography with the **best-rated weather app**!
- [**UV LENS**](https://www.uvlens.com/): Stay updated on the **UV index** forecast wherever you are, and be safe in the sun!
- [**Surfline**](https://www.surfline.com/): If you're heading to the beach, you can trust the 30-year-old **wave forecasting** platform for the best underwater videography, live webcasting from top spots, and comprehensive surf reports. Or, if you've never tried water sports, the Surfline application could definitely inspire you to do so 🤙

## 🧘‍♀️ Health & Fitness Apps

- [**Freeletics**](https://www.freeletics.com/en/): Get fit for the beach, or workout on the beach with your own **AI fitness coach**! With Freeletics you can exercise anywhere and anytime you feel like it.
- [**Pocket Yoga**](https://www.pocketyoga.com/): Make the most of warm weather and discover the joy of practicing Yoga in nature. Feel the positive impact on your mind, body and spirit...
- **[Google Fit](https://www.google.com/fit/)**: Rely on Google to track your health on your phone or smart watch in summer. Plus, get extra motivation to stay healthy, active and fit!
- 🚰 **Water Reminder Apps**: There are several mobile applications, specifically built to remind you to stay hydrated. And they all do a good job at it, if you're not used to drinking water regularly. Providing your body with enough liquid is vital for your health, especially in hot weather.

## 🗺️ Top Outdoor Applications

- [**VoiceMap**](https://voicemap.me/): Discover new worlds wherever you are this summer season with the brilliant VoiceMap app, combining interactive navigation with audio tours❣️
- [**iNaturalist**](https://www.inaturalist.org/pages/seek_app): Learn more about the Flora and Fauna anywhere you go by simply scanning whatever catches your eye with your phone.
- [**Komoot**](https://www.komoot.com/): Get inspired, plan and share your outdoor summer adventures with Komoot >>>
