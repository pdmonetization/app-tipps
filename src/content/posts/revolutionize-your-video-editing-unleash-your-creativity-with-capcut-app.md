## Quick verdict

CapCut remains a capable cross-platform editor for short-form social video, but the product is no longer easy to describe as simply “free.” Useful editing tools remain available without a subscription, while premium effects, cloud capacity and many AI workflows sit behind Pro plans or separate usage credits.

## What the app does in 2026

CapCut runs on mobile, desktop and the web. Its appeal is speed: templates, captions, effects, filters, timeline editing and social-ready export are accessible without the learning curve of a traditional professional suite. CapCut has expanded heavily into AI-assisted creation, including image, voice, script and video tools, but individual AI actions may consume credits even when a user has a subscription.

## Main features

- Mobile, desktop and browser editing
- Templates, text, effects, filters and automatic captions
- 4K and watermark-free export on applicable Pro workflows
- Cloud project storage and cross-device work
- Generative AI tools that may require monthly credits

## Pricing and fees

CapCut says pricing varies by platform and region. Its January 2026 comparison listed an individual Pro plan at $19.99 monthly or $179.99 yearly in the referenced market. CapCut also documents monthly and annual subscriptions, regional trials, and separate AI credits. Check the in-app checkout because taxes, app-store pricing and included storage or credits can differ.

## Limitations and risks

Templates can make unrelated videos look alike, and automatic output still needs human review. Confirm that music, fonts, stock media and generated assets are licensed for your intended commercial use. Privacy-conscious users should review upload and cloud settings before sending client footage or personal media to online tools.

## Who should use it?

Best for social creators and small teams that value speed and mobile workflows. For long-form, collaborative or tightly controlled professional projects, compare export control, media management, licensing and recurring cost with a desktop editor before committing.

## App-Tipps recommendation

Check the live price, permissions and account terms on your own device before subscribing or moving money. Start with the free tier or shortest commitment where one is available, and keep screenshots or receipts for paid transactions.
