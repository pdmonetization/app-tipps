## **FAQs**:

### **Is Disney Plus available in 4K resolution?**

Yes, Disney Plus offers 4K resolution for select movies and series. You can enjoy crisp images and vibrant colors with their highest-quality streaming service.

### **Is there a limit to how many devices I can stream on?**

With Disney Plus, you can have up to four simultaneous streams per account. This means you can watch content on up to four devices simultaneously.

### **Can I customize my Disney Plus profile?**

Yes, you can create multiple profiles within your Disney Plus account. This allows users to have personalized recommendations and continue watching the list, so they can always find what they're looking for quickly and easily.

### **Does Disney Plus have parental controls?**

Yes, Disney Plus offers its own Profiles & Parental Controls feature, allowing parents to set viewing limits and restrict specific content from their children's accounts. This ensures younger users are only watching age-appropriate programming.

### **Is Disney Plus free?**

No, Disney Plus is not free. The subscription costs $6.99 a month or $69.99 per year and allows access to all the content available on the streaming home service.

### **Is Disney Plus better than Netflix?**

The answer to this question depends on the user's preference. Disney Plus offers a vast library of movies and series from all the major studios, including Disney, Pixar, marvel cinematic universe , and more. On the other hand, Netflix caters to a broader range of content - from original programming to classic films. Ultimately the choice is up to you!
