> **Updated and fact-checked:** 14 August 2026. Features, prices and availability can vary by country, account and platform.

## Quick verdict

MrSpeedy is not the current global brand: the service rebranded as Borzo. Borzo can be useful for rapid intra-city documents and parcels in supported markets, but price and delivery promise depend on city, distance, vehicle and item.

## What it offers in 2026

Customers enter pickup and drop-off details, choose an available delivery type, receive a quote and track the courier. Borzo markets same-day and express local delivery for personal and business use; service regions differ and older MrSpeedy apps or pages should not be treated as current.

## Main features

- On-demand intra-city courier quotes
- Pickup and drop-off scheduling
- Courier tracking and status updates
- Different vehicle or parcel options by market
- Business delivery features in supported countries

## Pricing and fees

Charges are calculated from route, urgency, parcel and local demand. Obtain the in-app quote and inspect insurance or liability limits before sending valuable goods. Recipient changes or waiting time may cost extra.

## Limitations and risks

Check prohibited items, packaging and proof-of-delivery rules. Do not send cash, irreplaceable documents or fragile valuables without confirming coverage. Use only the official Borzo domain or store listing.

## Who is it best for?

Best for time-sensitive local deliveries in a covered city. Traditional tracked parcel services may be better for long-distance, high-value or non-urgent shipments.

## Our practical recommendation

Check the live checkout, permissions and cancellation terms on your own device. Use the free tier, trial or shortest plan first where possible, and keep transaction records for paid services.
