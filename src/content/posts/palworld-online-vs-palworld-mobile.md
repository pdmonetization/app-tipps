**Palworld Online vs Palworld Mobile** is not a comparison between two names for the same app. They are separate officially licensed games, made by different developers and built around different presentations of the Palworld universe.

Garena is developing Palworld Online as a mobile MMORPG with a persistent shared world, original story and cooperative settlements. PUBG Studios, part of Krafton, is developing Palworld Mobile as a separate mobile adaptation focused on survival, combat, crafting and playing together.

Neither project is simply Pocketpair publishing the existing game on an app store, and there is no announced save transfer or cross-progression between them.

## Who is making Palworld Online?

Garena is developing and publishing Palworld Online under license from Pocketpair. Garena's [August 3 announcement](https://www.garena.sg/news/7UPUTA) describes it as a new multiplayer survival adventure that expands the franchise into a mobile MMO ecosystem.

Its design is explicitly social. Players will meet in an interconnected open world, form groups, build shared settlements, hunt rare Pals, fight bosses and participate in both cooperative PvE and competitive PvP.

Garena has also confirmed original story elements and side stories that belong to this version. That is important: Palworld Online is not merely a server mode for the original Palworld. It is a distinct licensed game with its own narrative and progression environment.

## Who is making Palworld Mobile?

Palworld Mobile is being developed by PUBG Studios, the Krafton studio associated with PUBG. The [official Palworld Mobile website](https://palworldmobile.com/en) credits PUBG Studios and Pocketpair and presents four core pillars: survival, combat, crafting and playing together.

Its promotional description stays close to the original game's familiar actions. Players survive harsh conditions, capture Pals, fight with them, gather materials, craft tools and buildings, automate work and participate in multiplayer activities.

Krafton has not described Palworld Mobile as the same product as Palworld Online. The separate official site, developer and presentation confirm that it is an independent project.

## The biggest difference is the world structure

Palworld Online is being sold on scale and persistence. Garena emphasizes a seamless shared ecosystem where players encounter one another, create group settlements and progress as communities. That is traditional MMO framing even though the exact server population and channel system are unknown.

Palworld Mobile's official material focuses more heavily on reproducing the moment-to-moment survival fantasy: aiming and shooting, sending Pals into combat, building production systems, breeding creatures and challenging bosses or other players.

It would be premature to call Palworld Mobile a private-world game or say it lacks MMO-scale spaces. Krafton has not published enough detail for that conclusion. The accurate distinction is that Garena has formally labeled and described its game as an MMO, while PUBG Studios currently presents a broader mobile adaptation.

## Which game appears closer to the original Palworld?

Based on the current official descriptions, Palworld Mobile appears to emphasize a familiar translation of the original gameplay loop. Its site highlights survival pressure, direct combat, crafting chains, Pal labor, breeding and multiplayer—systems existing players already associate with Pocketpair's game.

Palworld Online preserves many of those systems but reorganizes them around a shared social world. Cooperative settlements, encounters with other groups, MMO progression and an original narrative could make it feel more like a spin-off than a portable edition.

That assessment may change after extended gameplay footage appears. Marketing pillars explain intent, not the final balance of combat, building, exploration and monetization.

## Will Palworld Online and Palworld Mobile share progress?

No shared account, character transfer, Pal transfer, inventory transfer or cross-progression system has been announced.

Because they are made and operated by different companies, shared progress would require an explicit commercial and technical agreement. A Pocketpair license does not automatically connect user databases, purchases or servers.

Plan as if each game will have a separate account and progression path unless both companies announce otherwise. Do not spend money or enter account credentials based on an unofficial promise that items will transfer.

## Will the two mobile games have different monetization?

Neither company has provided enough information for a reliable comparison. Palworld Online's 2026 announcement does not say whether it will be free-to-play, premium, subscription-based or supported by in-app purchases. Palworld Mobile's official site also does not provide a complete commercial model.

The developers' previous catalogs cannot answer the question. Garena and Krafton operate major free-to-play games, but that history is not proof of a particular battle pass, gacha system, stamina gate or price in these projects.

Monetization should be compared only when official store pages disclose:

1. Upfront price
2. In-app purchase ranges
3. Randomized item mechanics
4. Battle-pass or subscription terms
5. Advertising, if any
6. Regional price differences

Until then, claims that either game is definitely pay-to-win are opinions rather than verified product information.

## Which project is likely to launch first?

Palworld Online has the clearer public window: Garena says it is scheduled for 2026, although no exact date has been announced. Our [Palworld Online release-date tracker](/palworld-online-release-date/) records the confirmed timeline and registration status.

Palworld Mobile has an official update-subscription website, but a firm global launch date is not displayed there. Store or regional testing plans may appear before a worldwide release, so the first playable version may not be available in every country simultaneously.

Do not treat a leaked date, placeholder store date or third-party countdown as final. Mobile launches frequently use soft launches and staged regional rollouts.

## Which Palworld mobile game should you choose?

The choice depends on what the final products deliver, but the confirmed direction suggests a useful split.

Choose **Palworld Online** if you want:

- An MMO built around meeting other players
- Shared settlements and group progression
- Cooperative world bosses and organized PvP
- A new narrative setting and exclusive side stories
- Touch controls designed around large-scale mobile play

Watch **Palworld Mobile** if you want:

- A presentation closer to the original survival-crafting fantasy
- Direct combat alongside captured Pals
- Crafting, automation and breeding emphasized as core pillars
- A separate multiplayer interpretation from PUBG Studios
- More information before committing to an MMO-style service

Those are reasons to follow each project, not final purchase recommendations. Controls, performance, monetization, server stability and progression pacing will matter more than the announcement language once both games are playable.

## How to avoid downloading the wrong game

Two similar names create an easy opportunity for impersonation. Before installing anything:

1. Check whether the page says **Garena** for Palworld Online or **PUBG Studios/Krafton** for Palworld Mobile.
2. Reach the listing from the official developer site or verified social account.
3. Confirm that Pocketpair licensing is disclosed.
4. Avoid APK files advertised before an official Android listing is available.
5. Never use your Steam, Xbox or PlayStation password on an unofficial registration form.
6. Treat promises of shared inventories or free premium Pals as suspicious unless announced officially.

## Palworld Online vs Palworld Mobile: the current verdict

Palworld Online currently has the clearer identity and launch window. Garena has explained how its shared MMO world, cooperative bases, PvE, PvP and original stories fit together. That makes it easier to understand even though key commercial details are missing.

Palworld Mobile may ultimately feel closer to the original game, but PUBG Studios has revealed less about its release, world structure and account model. It deserves attention, not invented certainty.

The most important conclusion is simple: these are two separate licensed games. Follow them separately, expect separate progression, and wait for official store pages before choosing where to invest time or money.
