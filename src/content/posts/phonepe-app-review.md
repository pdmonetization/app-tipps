> **Updated and fact-checked:** 14 August 2026. App features, prices and availability can change by country and platform.

## Quick verdict

PhonePe is a broad payments app built primarily for India. It is useful for everyday UPI transfers, merchant QR payments, bills and mobile recharges, while additional services such as insurance depend on eligibility and location. International readers should not assume it works like a global remittance wallet.

## What the app does in 2026

The app lets eligible users link supported bank accounts to UPI, send money, pay merchants, recharge mobile or DTH services and pay utility bills. PhonePe has also added UPI Circle, which can let a primary user delegate payment access to a trusted secondary user, and in 2026 announced biometric authentication for eligible UPI transactions using a phone’s native fingerprint or face security.

## Main features

- UPI person-to-person transfers and merchant QR payments
- Mobile, DTH and utility-bill payments
- UPI Circle for delegated payments to trusted contacts
- Biometric UPI authorization on supported devices and transactions
- Insurance and other financial products where available

## Pricing and fees

Many ordinary UPI payments are presented as free to consumers, but fees, taxes or partner charges may apply to specific products and payment methods. Insurance, credit and investment products have their own terms. Review the amount and provider on the confirmation screen instead of assuming every PhonePe service has the same pricing.

## Limitations and risks

PhonePe is not a reason to weaken payment security. Verify the recipient name before approving a UPI request, never share a UPI PIN or one-time code, and do not enter a PIN merely to receive money. Biometric approval is convenient but still authorizes a real transaction. Service availability and limits depend on NPCI rules, the linked bank, account status and user location.

## Who should use it?

Best for eligible users in India who want one app for routine UPI and bill-payment tasks. People who only need simple transfers should compare it with their bank’s app and other UPI apps based on reliability, support and privacy—not cashback alone.

## App-Tipps recommendation

Check the live price, permissions and account terms on your own device before subscribing or moving money. Start with the free tier or shortest commitment where one is available, and keep screenshots or receipts for paid transactions.
