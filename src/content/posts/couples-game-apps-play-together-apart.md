Valentine's Day is the perfect occasion to discover fun couples apps and embark on a new journey together... from your phones❣️

Here are some of the best game apps for two:

## [Plato](https://platoapp.com/)

![Couples games app plato](/images/2025/02/Couples_games_app_Plato.png)

Play, connect and reconnect with your special someone in a 'plato' of free online games! Choose from various, relatively easy games with a turn-based mode, like Uno and Pool, with up to 24 hours to play back.

*Download Plato for FREE on the [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/plato-fun-multiplayer-games/id1054747306) and [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.plato.android)*

  

## [Minecraft](https://www.minecraft.net/)

![Couples games app minecraft 2](/images/2025/02/Couples_games_app_Minecraft_2.png)

Bond over crafting little builds together, or create a whole new world for the two of you, in Minecraft! You can also leave signs with messages for your other half to read. What an original way to express your feelings, don't you think? 😀

*Buy the Minecraft app on [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Mojang-Minecraft/dp/B00992CF6W), [Google Play,](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mojang.minecraftpe) and [the AppStore](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/minecraft-play-with-friends/id479516143)*

  

## 'Would You Rather' quiz apps

![Would You Rather apps for couples](/images/2025/02/Couples_games_apps_quizzes.png)

Whether you want to learn more about your love interest, or find surprising info about your long-term partner, you're sure to get lots of laughs, playing 'Would you rather'!

*Check out the 'Would you rather choose?' app for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dh3games.wouldyourather), or 'What would you choose? Rather' for adults 17+, available on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=macrostudios.wouldyourather&hl=en&gl=MX) and [Apple](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/what-would-you-choose-rather/id1216673574).*

  

## [Monopoly GO!](/monopoly-go-goes-crazy/)

![MonopolyGO game app, suitable for couples](/images/2025/02/Couples_games_app_MonopolyGO.png)

Stick to the classics and roll the dice on 'Monopoly GO!' - the relatively new app of the century-old game. Discover secret treasures, sticker gifts, daily treats, and lots of fun time together!

*Download Monopoly GO! for free on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.scopely.monopolygo) and [the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/monopoly-go/id1621328561)*

  

## PAIRED

![Couples app paired](/images/2025/02/Couples_app_Paired.png)

"Stay in love with [Paired](https://www.paired.com/)"💕- a comprehensive couples app, packed with relevant games, quizzes, relationship advice, important reminders and so much more you didn't even know you needed...

*Get Paired for two on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.getpaired.app) or [the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/paired-couples-relationship/id1469609343)*
