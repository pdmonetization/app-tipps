## Quick verdict

Traveloka is a strong all-in-one booking option in its core Asian markets. Its app is convenient for comparing flights, hotels, airport transport and activities, but travellers must still read the supplier’s cancellation and baggage rules.

## What it offers in 2026

The app supports flights, accommodation, attractions and other travel products depending on country. Useful tools include price alerts, app-only offers, local payment options, booking notifications and eligible refund or reschedule flows.

## Main features

- Flight and hotel search and booking
- Activities and airport transport in supported markets
- Price alerts and app-only promotions
- Digital itinerary and travel notifications
- Refund or reschedule tools for eligible bookings

## Pricing and fees

The app is free, while booking prices, taxes, baggage, resort fees and change costs come from the selected product and supplier. A coupon may have dates, payment-method or minimum-spend conditions. Compare the final payable total.

## Limitations and risks

Traveloka is an intermediary for many bookings. Record the fare rules, supplier reference and support conversation. Confirm passport names, baggage and visa requirements independently; “easy reschedule” is not available for every rate.

## Who is it best for?

Best for travellers in markets where Traveloka offers local payments and support. Compare flexible and non-refundable prices based on the risk of plans changing.

## Our practical recommendation

Check the live checkout, permissions and cancellation terms on your own device. Use the free tier, trial or shortest plan first where possible, and keep transaction records for paid services.
