## **What exactly is Apple Arcade?**

Apple Arcade is a game subscription service that gives users access to over 100 games from leading game developers across all platforms and devices. For only $4.99 per month, users can enjoy the latest titles on their iOS, Mac, and tvOS devices.

The library of Apple Arcade games includes genres such as adventure, arcade, puzzle-solving, and racing. Every month the library of games grows as new titles are added and existing ones receive updates.

## **Apple Arcade: Is it Worth Subscribing?**

Subscribing to Apple Arcade may be a great option for gamers looking to get the most out of their gaming experience. ## **How to Subscribe to Apple Arcade?**

1. Open the App Store on your iOS device, or sign into iCloud on your Mac.

2. Go to the Arcade tab and select “Try Apple Arcade”

3. Select a payment method for your subscription: you can use an existing Apple ID balance, credit card, debit card, or PayPal account to pay for your subscription.

4. Choose your subscription plan: either pay monthly or annually, with discounts available for the latter option.

5. On the following page you can select which games you want to include in your subscription and begin enjoying Apple Arcade!

Once you’ve subscribed to Apple Arcade, you will be able to access the library of games through the Arcade tab on your device, allowing you to play any game in the Apple Arcade lineup.

Every month new titles will be added to the service, offering subscribers an ever-growing selection of games. You can also unsubscribe at any time if you decide that Apple Arcade isn’t for you.

## **FAQs**

### **What do I get with an Apple Arcade subscription?**

With an Apple Arcade subscription service you get unlimited access to over 100 award-winning games from leading game developers, available across all platforms and devices such as iOS, Mac, and tvOS. You can also access offline play and additional exclusive content.

### **Is there a trial version?**

Yes! Apple offers a 1-month free trial of the service, so you can try out all the games before committing to a subscription.

### **What is the point of Apple Arcade?**

Apple Arcade is meant to provide users with a convenient, all-in-one gaming experience. By offering access to over 100 award-winning games from leading developers and additional exclusive content, Apple aims to give customers the best mobile games experience at an affordable price.

### **Who can use an Apple Arcade subscription?**

Anyone with an Apple device can use an Apple Arcade subscription. Whether you have an iPhone, iPad, Mac, or tvOS device, you can access the over 100 games and additional exclusive content with your subscription.

### **Is it worth paying for Apple Arcade?**

That depends on what you're looking for in a gaming service. If you're looking for unlimited access to award-winning games from leading developers and additional exclusive content, then Apple Arcade might be worth it.  
The service also offers offline play and is conveniently available across all platforms and devices, so if these features are important to you then the subscription could provide enough value to justify its cost.

### **Can I still play Apple Arcade games without subscription?**

Yes, some games that are available on Apple Arcade can also be purchased individually. However, you won't have access to the full set of features, additional exclusive content, or offline play that is included with the subscription. If you're looking for the full Apple Arcade experience, then a subscription will be necessary.

### **Is an Apple music subscription also available with apple arcade?**

While Apple Arcade and Apple Music are both subscription services offered by Apple, they are different services with different features. Apple Music is a streaming music service that provides access to millions of songs, while Apple Arcade is a gaming subscription with over 100 award-winning games.
