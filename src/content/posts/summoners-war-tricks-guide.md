Summoners War: Sky Arena is an Android and iOS game where players collect monsters and enter RPG combat in a beautiful 3D world. Every monster in Summoners War boasts unique skills and properties which allow it to take on various combat roles, like a tank role, a support role, an attack role, and combination roles, with further customization evolving from the game's rune system.

## Tips and Tricks to *Summoners War: Sky Arena*

1. ***Know Which Monster Attributes Are Strong and Weak Against One Another***

Every monster is associated with one particular element. You should always know which fundamental properties are strong and weak against its contemporaries. There is Fire, Water, Wind, Light, and Dark. Water is powerful against Fire, Fire is strong against Wind, Wind is strong against Water, and Light/Darkness is weak against each other.

![Summoners war sky arena ipv 5](/images/2023/02/Summoners-War-Sky-Arena-ipv-5.jpg)

2. ***You Should Focus on Attacking One Enemy at a Time***

Focus your attacking efforts on taking down one monster at a time. This tactic will make your life much easier as you will knock down monsters one after one and give them less of a chance of making a comeback.

3. ***Boss Fights Require Your Best Monsters and Tactics***

Boss characters will most certainly provide you with the toughest challenges. Build up a party of monsters that have an elemental type that is strong against that boss. Bring your online friend's leader monster into battle with you and use your most powerful abilities.

4. ***Participate in the Arena Battles as Much as Possible***

When you participate in the arena Battle, you will earn a lot of rewards, such as crystals and essential items for your monsters. Try to take on the fewer difficult parties that reside at the Arena. The higher your ranking once you complete an event, the better your rewards you will have. Keep competing in the Arena for more EXP. Buy the Arena defenses to increase your chances of success.

![Summoners war sky arena ipv 1](/images/2023/02/Summoners-War-Sky-Arena-ipv-1.jpg)

5. ***Utilize Angelmon and Devilmon to the Fullest***

Devilmon and Angelmon are Material-type monsters. Devilmons can be found through the Devilmon Cave and Glory Shop, while Angelmons can be located within the Angel Garden and elemental dungeons. Devilmon can boost the features of any of your monster's skills, while Angelmon can gift your monsters with tons of EXP when they are used as material. You should make it a habit to use certain elemental Angelmon with monsters of the same type. Rainbow Angelmon works well with any elemental creature in your party.

6. ***Construct the Types of Environmental Locales That Gift Your Monsters with Extra Experience***

Expanding your land with these three locations will guarantee your monsters receive EXP bonuses – the Tranquil Forest, Gusty Cliffs, and Crystal Lake. You can place up to four of your monsters within the Tranquil Forest, and they will come away with 100 extra experience points for every hour that passes. As for the Crystal Lake and Gusty Cliffs, your monsters will earn 150 EXP for every hour (four monsters can be stored in the Crystal Lake, while five monsters can be placed at the Gusty Cliffs).

7. ***Complete The Kabir Ruins ASAP to Move onto the Tougher Cairo's Dungeon***

Once you are strong enough, finish everything that resides within the Kabir Ruins. After you are done with this section, Cairo's Dungeon will become unlocked. While the Cairo Dungeon is very challenging, entering them will lead to the discovery of the best items for monster awakenings, rare runes, and other types of bonus goodies.

![Summoners war sky arena ipv 3](/images/2023/02/Summoners-War-Sky-Arena-ipv-3.jpg)

8. ***Make Sure You Raise Up a Powerful Healer***

Having a strong Healer on your team is pretty much a necessity. The Fairy-type characters in the game, such as monsters like Neal and Lulu, have the stats and abilities needed to heal your party and head into battle with strong attacks. Putting them in the front is a good move since they have a lot of health and can operate as tank characters and healers. Lulu is one of the best healers, Fairy characters in the game. Make upgrades on these fairy characters.
