**Palworld Online vs Palworld** is the difference between a mobile MMO spin-off and Pocketpair's original open-world survival game. They share Pals, exploration, crafting, building and combat, but they are separate products made by different studios for different world structures.

Pocketpair develops the released Palworld for PC and consoles. Garena is developing Palworld Online under license as a phone-first shared world with cooperative settlements, PvE, PvP and an original story.

The mobile game is not a port, expansion or companion client. No save transfer, crossplay or purchase sharing has been announced.

## Palworld Online vs Palworld at a glance

| Detail | Palworld Online | Original Palworld |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Developer | Garena | Pocketpair |
| Status | In development | Full release launched July 2026 |
| Main platforms | Mobile | PC, Xbox, PlayStation and Mac versions exist |
| World structure | Shared MMO ecosystem | Solo, co-op and dedicated/community worlds |
| Story | New setting and original side stories | Pocketpair's main Palworld setting |
| Multiplayer | Group progression, PvE and PvP | Co-op, dedicated servers and configurable PvP |

| Save transfer | Not announced | Saves belong to the original game's ecosystem |

The table describes what has been announced, not every feature the mobile game may eventually include. Garena has not yet published its complete server, account or monetization design.

## What stays familiar in Palworld Online?

Garena is retaining the franchise's recognizable loop. Players explore a large world with Pals, capture and raise creatures, gather materials, craft equipment, build settlements and survive environmental or enemy threats.

Pals remain more than battle companions. They can support production, automate work and help defend a base. Open-world bosses, rare-Pal hunts and cooperative exploration also carry forward the idea that different creatures solve different practical problems.

That shared foundation is why the mobile project still deserves the Palworld name. The largest changes concern ownership, population and progression rather than removing the core survival and creature systems.

## The major change is private worlds versus an MMO world

The original Palworld can be played solo, in small online co-op groups or on dedicated and community servers. Pocketpair's [official server guide](https://docs.palworldgame.com/) explains how hosts configure their own worlds, player limits and server rules.

Palworld Online is described as a seamless shared ecosystem. Players are expected to encounter others naturally, form groups, create communal settlements and take part in organized cooperative or competitive activities.

That shift affects more than server size. A persistent MMO normally needs rules for land, shared storage, construction permissions, matchmaking, maintenance and live events. Garena has confirmed the communal direction but not the exact implementation.

Players who value a private world with adjustable settings should not assume Palworld Online will offer the same control. Players who find privately hosted worlds lonely or difficult to organize may prefer the MMO structure.

## Who develops each game?

Pocketpair created and continues to develop the original Palworld. Its full Steam release arrived on July 9, 2026 after the early-access period that began in January 2024. The [official Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1623730/Palworld/) identifies Pocketpair as both developer and publisher.

Garena develops and publishes Palworld Online through an official license. Pocketpair CEO Takuro Mizobe publicly welcomed the collaboration, but a license does not make the two development teams interchangeable.

This matters for support and updates. Questions about the original game's balance, servers and saves belong to Pocketpair. Palworld Online launch, accounts, monetization and mobile performance belong to Garena.

There is also a separate PUBG Studios project called Palworld Mobile. If that sounds unnecessarily confusing, our [Palworld Online vs Palworld Mobile guide](/palworld-online-vs-palworld-mobile/) maps the two mobile games directly.

## How will multiplayer differ?

The original Palworld supports solo play, online co-op and dedicated servers. Its Steam listing describes four-player online co-op and dedicated worlds supporting more players, while server configuration lets communities change many rules.

Garena has confirmed these Palworld Online activities:

1. Meeting other players in the open world
2. Forming groups
3. Building shared settlements
4. Cooperative PvE encounters
5. Open-world boss fights
6. Rare-Pal hunts
7. Competitive PvP matches
8. Visiting other players' bases

Party size, server capacity, guild limits and competitive modes remain unknown. It is also unclear whether a player can reserve a private space or experience most of the story alone.

The safe conclusion is that Palworld Online makes social play the default design rather than an optional layer added to a personal world.

## Will combat and controls be the same?

The original is designed around controllers, mouse and keyboard, with direct third-person movement, aiming, weapons and Pal commands. Its interface can dedicate many buttons to building and inventory management.

Palworld Online uses a touchscreen-first interface. Garena has confirmed optional one-tap controls for planning bases, constructing and relocating buildings, and deploying Pals. This could reduce menu friction on a smaller screen.

Garena has not shown enough final combat footage to compare aiming precision, dodge timing, weapon handling or Pal command depth. It also has not confirmed controller support. A mobile-friendly interface may be more convenient for management while still offering less granular control than the original game.

Any article claiming that combat is better or worse before a public test is reviewing a promise, not a playable product.

## Can Palworld saves move to Palworld Online?

No save import or cross-progression has been announced. The products have different developers, world structures and original story content, making a direct transfer unlikely unless the companies build a specific conversion system.

Do not expect to bring over:

- A character or level
- Captured Pals
- Bases and stored resources
- Technology unlocks
- World settings
- Purchased copies or platform entitlements
- Mods or community-server data

Even a shared Pocketpair account would not automatically mean compatible save files. Treat Palworld Online as a fresh start until Garena confirms otherwise.

## Will Palworld Online support mods or private servers?

Garena has not announced either feature. The original Palworld supports a configurable server ecosystem, and its PC community can use mods. A live mobile MMO normally gives the operator tighter control over client files, economies and world state, but that general pattern is not proof of Garena's final policy.

Players who consider private servers, custom settings or mods essential should continue with the original game until explicit support appears. Palworld Online's current selling point is an accessible managed world, not server ownership.

## Which version is better for solo players?

The original Palworld is the safer choice today. It is released, supports solo play and lets a player control the pace and world settings without depending on a public MMO population.

Palworld Online may still support substantial solo progression. Garena mentions exploration, stories, base planning and creature collecting, all of which can work alone. However, its public description repeatedly prioritizes teams, shared settlements and social encounters. Offline play has not been announced.

If you want short phone sessions but dislike mandatory groups, wait for beta reports explaining which objectives require other players.

## Which version should a new player choose?

Choose the original Palworld now if you have compatible hardware and want a complete survival game with private-world control, established guides and no need to wait for an uncertain mobile launch.

Wait for Palworld Online if you specifically want:

- Native mobile controls
- A fresh server where everyone begins together
- MMO communities and shared settlements
- Regular cooperative encounters
- Structured PvP
- An original mobile-only narrative

The two games can coexist because they solve different problems. Palworld offers control and an established sandbox. Palworld Online promises accessibility and persistent community play.

## Palworld Online vs Palworld: current verdict

Palworld Online is not replacing Palworld. It is extending the franchise into a different service model and device category. The MMO may become the easiest way to play with a large community on a phone, while the original remains the stronger choice for private worlds, server configuration and a proven survival sandbox.

There is not enough evidence to score the mobile game or recommend spending money. There is enough information to say who it is for: players who accept a fresh start and want the Palworld concept rebuilt around a persistent mobile community.
