In today's fast-paced world, staying organized and productive is crucial. Thankfully, with the rise of mobile apps, it's easier than ever to keep track of our daily routines and achieve our goals. ***Here are the top daily routine planner apps to try in 2023.***

## **Habitify**

![Habitify](/images/2023/06/Habitify.png)

**Download Habitify App for:**

[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.unstatic.habitify&hl=en&gl=US)

[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habitify-habit-tracker-goal/id1111447047)

This habit-tracking app allows users to set daily goals and reminders for healthy habits like drinking water, exercising, and meditating. It offers customizable reminders and statistics to track progress, making it an excellent tool for establishing and maintaining new habits. The app is available for free on iOS and Android, with a premium subscription that offers additional features.

**Key features:** Habit tracking, goal setting, reminders, progress tracking, streaks, and customizable habits.

**Pros**: User-friendly interface, customizable features, and a range of available habits to track.

**Cons**: Limited free version, expensive premium version, no option for habit categories.

**Pricing**: Free version available, Premium starts at $4.99/month, $35.99/year.

**Compatibility**: [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habitify-habit-tracker-goal/id1111447047), [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.unstatic.habitify&hl=en&gl=US), Web.

## **Todoist**

![Todoist](/images/2023/06/Todoist.png)

**Download Todoist App for:**

[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.todoist&hl=en&gl=US)

[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/todoist-to-do-list-planner/id572688855)

Todoist is a popular to-do list app that offers personalized goal-setting and productivity tracking features. It allows users to assign tasks to specific projects, set deadlines, and prioritize tasks based on importance. The app is available for free on iOS and Android, with a premium subscription that offers additional features such as reminders and productivity tracking.

**Key features:** To-do list, goal setting, productivity tracking, project management, collaboration features, and reminders.

**Pros:** User-friendly interface, ability to organize tasks into projects, and the ability to set recurring tasks.

**Cons:** Some advanced features require a premium account, no calendar view, and limited customization options.

**Pricing:** Free version available, Premium starts at $3/month, $36/year.

**Compatibility:** [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/todoist-to-do-list-planner/id572688855), [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.todoist&hl=en&gl=US), Web, Windows, macOS, Chrome, and Firefox.

## **Trello**

![Trello app](/images/2023/06/trello-app.png)

**Download Trello App for:**

[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.trello&hl=en&gl=US)

[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trello-organize-anything/id461504587)

Trello is a project management app that uses a card-based system to help users prioritize tasks and track progress on complex projects. It allows users to create boards, lists, and cards to organize their tasks and collaborate with team members. The app is available for free on iOS and Android, with a premium subscription that offers additional features such as unlimited boards and power-ups.

**Key features:** Project management, task management, collaboration features, card-based interface, and the ability to add attachments and labels.

**Pros**: User-friendly interface, flexibility in organizing tasks, and integration with other productivity tools.

**Cons:** Limited customization options, no calendar view, and lack of progress tracking features.

**Pricing:** Free version available, Business Class starts at $9.99/user/month, Enterprise pricing available.

**Compatibility:** [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trello-organize-anything/id461504587), [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.trello&hl=en&gl=US), Web.

## **Google Keep**

![Google new](/images/2023/06/google-new.jpg)

**Download Google Keep App for:**

[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.keep&hl=en&gl=US)

[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-keep-notes-and-lists/id1029207872)

Google Keep is a note-taking app that also offers task-list and reminder features. It allows users to create notes, checklists, and reminders that can be color-coded for easy organization. The app also includes voice-to-text transcription and image recognition features, making it an excellent tool for capturing ideas and inspiration on the go. The app is available for free on iOS and Android.

**Key features:** Note-taking, task-list, reminder features, voice-to-text transcription, color-coding, and collaboration features.

**Pros:** User-friendly interface, ability to collaborate and share notes, and integration with other Google products.

**Cons:** Limited customization options, no progress tracking features, and lack of advanced features for productivity tracking.

**Pricing:** Free.

**Compatibility:** [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-keep-notes-and-lists/id1029207872), [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.keep&hl=en&gl=US), Web.

## **Forest**

![Forest](/images/2023/06/Forest.png)

**Download Forest App for:**

[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cc.forestapp&hl=en&gl=US)

[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/forest-focus-for-productivity/id866450515)

Forest is a productivity app that uses gamification to encourage users to stay focused and avoid distractions. Users plant virtual trees that grow as they stay on task, with the option to customize the time intervals and earn rewards for completing tasks. The app also includes a social feature that allows users to compete with friends and family members. The app is available for a one-time fee on iOS and Android.

**Key features:** Productivity tracking, gamification, focus timer, and the ability to plant virtual trees.

**Pros:** Unique approach to productivity tracking, ability to customize focus sessions, and motivation from the gamification aspect.

**Cons:** Limited features beyond focus timer, lack of task organization features, and no free version.

**Pricing:** $1.99.

**Compatibility:** [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/forest-focus-for-productivity/id866450515), [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cc.forestapp&hl=en&gl=US).

## **Comparison**

In comparing the daily routine planner apps, it is important to consider several factors, including ease of use, customization options, and integration with other apps and devices.

Ease of use is a crucial factor when it comes to daily routine planner apps. The app should be intuitive and easy to navigate, with clear instructions and a user-friendly interface. Apps like **Google Keep** and **Todoist** have a straightforward interface that is easy to use even for beginners, while apps like **Trello** and **Habitify** may require more time to master due to their more complex features.

Customization options are also important, as they allow users to tailor the app to their specific needs and preferences. Apps like **Trello** and **Todoist** offer a wide range of customization options, allowing users to create custom tags, labels, and categories to organize their tasks and projects. **Forest**, on the other hand, offers a limited range of customization options, focusing more on providing a unique and engaging user experience.

Integration with other apps and devices is another important factor to consider. Some apps, such as **Google Keep** and **Todoist**, have seamless integration with other Google services and devices, making it easy to access and manage tasks and notes across different platforms. **Trello**, on the other hand, offers integration with a wide range of third-party apps and services, making it a more versatile option for users who rely on multiple tools to manage their daily routines.

Ultimately, the best daily routine planner app will depend on the user's specific needs and preferences. Some users may prefer a simple and straightforward app like **Google Keep** or **Todoist**, while others may benefit from the more advanced features offered by apps like **Trello** or **Habitify**. It is important to consider all the factors discussed above and choose an app that aligns with your goals and lifestyle.
