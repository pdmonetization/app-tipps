Today, there is an app for everything, and more! In 2025, some apps can literally help save your own life, or the life of a loved one... Here are some mobile application, we feel, can genuinely do that:

## Offline Survival Manual application

![Offline Survival Manual mobile app for Android](/images/2025/04/Offline-survival-manual-app-logo-png.webp)

If you're ever lost in the woods, or stuck in a remote area for any reason, having a comprehensive survival manual at your fingertips could be your salvation! The **'Offline Survival Manual' app by ligi** has been highly recommended by tens of thousands of users, who took advantage of it during crisis, or when simply trekking and camping in the great outdoors❣

You too can [download the FREE Offline Survival Manual on an Android phone](https://play.google.com/store/apps/dev?id=5229070112536767347) to get inspired for your next trip in nature, and be even better prepared, just in case!

  

## Noonlight

![Noonlight personal alarm app](/images/2025/04/Noonlight-app-logo-2-png.webp)

The Noonlight app acts as a **quiet alarm**, you can trigger from your mobile. If you feel scared, unsafe, or threatened by anyone, Noonlight enables you to call emergency services in just a few clicks without having to explain anything on the phone. And with its latest update in April 2025, you can now request help even faster than before through the new 'Fast Forward' option in the app.

Get Noonlight on an [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.safetrekapp.safetrek) or [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/noonlight-feel-protected-24-7/id716262008), and **feel protected 24/7**!

  

## Snug Safety

![Snug safe app logo png](/images/2025/04/Snug-Safe-app-logo-png.webp)

Snug Safety is a **daily check-in app**, designed for people who live alone. It's also ideal for checking on elderly people, as it is very straightforward and easy to use. It can be utilized as a medical alert with handy automated features. It's got both free and paid access options, depending on your needs.

Snug Safety is available on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=snugsafety.com.snugsafety) and the [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/snug-safety/id1122758716).

  

## Google Pixel Watch App

![Google Watch app for Android](/images/2025/04/Google-Pixel-Watch-app-logo-png.webp)

Google's revolutionary feature, called **'Loss of Pulse Detection'**, just got launched in the States, after FDA's approval in March 2025! It's activated through the Google Pixel Watch mobile application, but you would need the latest Google smartwatch (Pixel Watch 3) to benefit from it. The watch makes an automated call to 911 if its user's pulse can't be detected while wearing it, greatly increasing the chance of survival in case of a heart attack!
