## Quick verdict

UrbanflixTV remains an active niche streaming service rather than a discontinued app. It may appeal to viewers interested in its specific independent and culturally focused catalogue, but the smaller library makes a catalogue check more important than with a broad mainstream service.

## What the app does in 2026

UrbanflixTV offers subscription access through its website and apps, including mobile and several TV platforms. Its catalogue page highlights films and series rather than live television. Availability can vary by device and region, so confirm that the exact title and platform you want are supported before paying.

## Main features

- On-demand films and series
- Mobile apps plus listed TV-platform access
- Monthly, quarterly and annual membership choices
- Cross-device viewing with an active subscription
- A niche catalogue that differs from mass-market streaming libraries

## Pricing and fees

At the time of our check, the official join page listed $7.99 monthly, $20.99 quarterly and $79.99 annually. App-store listings can show different legacy or platform-specific in-app prices. The service renews automatically until cancelled, so use the checkout screen as the final price and note where the subscription must be managed.

## Limitations and risks

A low annual price is not good value if the catalogue has only a few titles you want. Check video quality, subtitles, casting support and simultaneous-stream rules for your devices. Catalogue rights can change, and app-store subscriptions may need to be cancelled through Apple or Google rather than the website.

## Who should use it?

Best for viewers who have already browsed the current catalogue and found several specific titles. Use the shortest plan first if you are unsure about playback quality or content depth.

## App-Tipps recommendation

Check the live price, permissions and account terms on your own device before subscribing or moving money. Start with the free tier or shortest commitment where one is available, and keep screenshots or receipts for paid transactions.
