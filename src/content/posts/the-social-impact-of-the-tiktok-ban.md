The [TikTok app](https://tiktok.pxf.io/c/2912724/2392239/27618 "Download TikTok app") is finally back on the app stores and available for download (get on [Android](https://tiktok.pxf.io/c/2912724/2420676/27618 "Download TikTok on Android") and [iOS](https://tiktok.pxf.io/c/2912724/2392239/27618 "Download TikTok on iPhone")).

It took the video platform, owned by the Chinese company [ByteDance](https://www.bytedance.com/en/), some time to come back after President Donald Trump signed the executive order, giving it an extra 75 days to comply with the law.

TikTok wasn't down for long, but its ban definitely created quite a stir in the social media world! It caused not only a massive wave of opinions, flooding the web, but it was literally **LIFE-CHANGING FOR SEVERAL OTHER SOCIAL APPS TOO:**

## RedNote app

A huge number of US TikTok users migrated to the 'Little Red Book' platform, following the announcement about the ban. The RedNote app was already extremely popular in China, and now it's triumphed in the States too, connecting Americans and Chinese people like never before!

**Get RedNote on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.xingin.xhs&hl=en) and [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rednote-share-connect-love/id741292507)**

## FLIP

Another video sharing app, that filled the gazing gap, left by the ban! Unlike TikTok, FLIP is focused mainly on the promotional aspect of online interaction, in the form of video reviews by real people.

**Download FLIP on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flipfit&hl=en) and [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flip-watch-create-shop/id1470077137)**
