## **Valkyrie Connect: Beginner Guide**

- **Action Skills**

These are skills unique to each character. By attacking or receiving hits from enemies and with the passage of time, each hero's action skill gauge will gradually fill up. Tap a filled gauge to activate a hero's action skill.

- **Gear Skills**

These are skills attached to gear. When equipment or accessories are equipped, these skills have a chance to activate in battle. A higher skill probably can give a higher chance for the skill to trigger and make the effect more powerful.

- **Limit Bursts**

The Limit Burst gauge will charge during a battle with the passage of time and when you use your heroes' acting skills. Tap the gauge when it fills, then tap one of your heroes to activate the skill. There are a total of ten different Limit skill Burst skills. They attack all enemies and have special effects. If you have multiple heroes in your party with the same Limit Burst, the effect will be more powerful.

- **Battle Terminology**
  - **Reflect:** Damage to an enemy with a given percentage of damage received to self.
  - **Absorb**: Heal HP equal to a given percentage of damage received.
  - **Leech**: Heal HP equal to a given percentage of damage dealt.
