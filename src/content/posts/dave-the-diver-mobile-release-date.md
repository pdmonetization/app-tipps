## Is Dave the Diver Mobile free?

It is **free to start, not completely free to play**. Both store descriptions say players can download the game and play the opening portion without charge, then unlock the full experience.

The US App Store currently displays a $9.99 in-app purchase for the complete game. Google Play confirms an in-app purchase but does not expose the local price on its public web listing before release.

This structure is closer to a playable demo followed by a premium purchase than a normal free-to-play economy. The official description does not advertise energy timers, randomized character pulls or recurring access payments.

Regional pricing can differ. Check the amount shown inside the official version on the account and storefront you actually use before approving the purchase.
