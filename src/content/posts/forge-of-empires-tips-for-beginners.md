Forge of Empires is a free-to-play 2D browser-based online strategy game from InnoGame, the creator of the classic Tribal War game. Forge of Empire gives players the chance to make their unique state city into an imperial powerhouse. Everyone has dreams of building an empire at some point or another, and Forge of Empires can bring that dream to life.

Players will watch how civilizations in Forge of Empires begin in the Stone Age and advance all the way into the Industrial Age. The campaign mode is single-player and brings players a unique, in-depth experience that enables them to use military action, skilled diplomacy, and cunning strategy to achieve their goals. Forge of Empires is available on a web-based browser, iOS and Android.

## **Tips and Tricks to *Forge of Empires***

1. **Watch and learn from better players.**

If you want to be successful, you have to learn from those who have already succeeded. It is not shaming to learn from the better ones. It is worth watching because it can be an inspiration for how to play. It is not worth insisting on a bad strategy if only it were “my strategy.” Better to be a fox – clever and cunning.

You have to observe better players and copy good ideas about them. It is worth watching a group of good players and seeking inspiration for your own game. You can also combine the ideas of different players and create your good idea.

Finally, you should be careful who you are watching. Even among the players who have a lot of points are hopeless cases with completely useless cities and great buildings. So it is very important to watch critically, duplicate the good schemes and avoid mistakes.

![Forge of empires ipv 1](/images/2023/02/forge-of-empires-ipv-1.jpg)

2. **Don’t be afraid to ask.**

Don’t lift the pride and honor; don’t be sure that you know everything. If you have a problem, then talk to other players, for example, on chat. Talk with the best players on the server, with colleagues from the. Even unknown players from the chat can serve your opinion, e.g., if it is worth bringing concrete Bs or how to cross the province. If someone else asks, do not be afraid to answer. Help others, and someone will surely help you.

3. **Plan and calculate**.

Forge of Empires is a strategy game. And as usual, in any strategy, you need a really good plan. There is nothing worse than playing disorganized, rash decisions and chaotic games. There is also nothing worse than the blind imitation of the better if something is currently not profitable for us. Many things in Forge of Empires require simply counting, e.g., which house is for us the most profitable, or whether the benefits of putting the GBs will be higher than the cost of its construction. Think long-term, plan your town in a few periods forward, save resources for later, and invest in things that will bring you long-term benefits. Count a lot at every opportunity. The cold calculation is always better than rushy, sometimes irreversible decisions.

4. **Join a guild; this is profitable**.

Even if you don’t play too often, join a guild. There are many players who pretend to be “singles.” Not everyone is a “herd animal,” but face it. Membership in the guild is, of course, an obligation, smaller or bigger, but there is always something to do. But it is also a good chance to get to know new players. Knowing new players is a chance for a good and cheap trade; colleagues help with different problems (the conquest of the province, removal of trade offers or scaffolding) or the motivation for buildings. It also accesses the GBs of other players, in which you can invest. It is also more fun out of the game. Guilds fighting in GvG, which have reached an appropriate level, provide daily free points from the town hall, lower building costs, and faster training troops.

![Forge of empires ipv 3](/images/2023/02/forge-of-empires-ipv-3.jpg)

5. **Find friends and get to know your neighbors; they will help you in developing**.

If the guild fails, you can always count on friends and neighbors. Friends are your opportunity to build a grid of regular contributors. Another GBs, motivations, and trade offer to which you have access. Feel free to use the help they can give you in the form of motivation when your happiness is down and you quickly need a click of the building. Just invite a volunteer from chat, and the problem is solved.

Neighbors are usually on the same level of development as you (similar age), so you can barter with them. It is a big advantage when you quickly need some goods. We can also motivate them and seek blueprints. Of course, there are bad neighbors who rob us. But most is usually less or more useful.

Friends and neighbors are also a chance to find a cool guild to which you may be invited due to your connections. It is an opportunity to talk, meet new people, and exchange ideas on the strategy of the game. Friends are very useful for permanent cooperation with trade. You can create useful links, for example, mutually provide each good to which we have a production bonus.
