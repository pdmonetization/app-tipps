The **No Man's Sky iOS release date** is October 28, 2026 in Hello Games' announcement, while the US App Store currently displays October 29. The iPhone and iPad version will let you try the beginning for free; its US listing shows a $19.99 one-time full-game unlock. As of October 19, it remains a pre-order, not a playable public release.

This guide checks the developer announcement and store listing as they stand before launch. For the hardware decision, use our [iPhone and iPad compatibility guide](/no-mans-sky-iphone-compatibility/); for the gameplay trade-offs, read [mobile versus PC and console](/no-mans-sky-mobile-vs-pc-console/).

## No Man's Sky iOS release date: October 28 or 29?

There are two dates to keep separate. [Hello Games' October 12 announcement](https://www.nomanssky.com/2026/10/no-mans-sky-ios/) names October 28. The [US App Store product page](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/no-mans-sky/id1615755735) shows an expected October 29 release. Neither source gives us a verified worldwide unlock timetable explaining that difference.

Treat October 28–29 as the current launch window, then use the listing on your own device for availability. We cannot responsibly turn the discrepancy into a claim about a midnight release, a particular time zone or a delay. An expected store date is also not proof that the game has actually become downloadable.

If the game has not appeared when you expect it, open the product page itself before looking for troubleshooting fixes. Reinstalling another app, changing a phone setting or buying a controller cannot make an unreleased listing available. First establish whether the release has reached your storefront.

## What is free, and what costs $19.99?

The store describes a free opening section followed by a one-time purchase for the complete game. The US in-app purchase entry is **FULL GAME UNLOCK — $19.99** when checked on October 19. That is a US storefront observation, not a worldwide price or a promise about later promotions.

The distinction matters if a search result calls the mobile version “free.” A free initial download does not mean unlimited access to the whole adventure. Likewise, a one-time unlock is different from a recurring subscription. Check the purchase sheet presented by your own Apple account before authorizing payment.

The listing does not specify the exact number of free minutes, a trial expiry countdown or a complete mission-by-mission cutoff. We therefore would not plan around finishing a particular storyline for free. Use the opening section to judge whether you want to continue, then read the actual unlock offer.

A useful personal buying rule is to identify your deciding factor before installing. Is it comfortable touch control, legible text, exploration on the device you already own, or portability? Trying those priorities is more informative than spending the entire opening section looking for a feature you will rarely use.

## Use the official iPhone and iPad download route

Open the [No Man's Sky App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/no-mans-sky/id1615755735), or follow its link from the developer announcement. Confirm that the seller is **Hello Games Ltd** and that you are looking at the native iPhone/iPad product. A similarly named guide, wallpaper app or cloud-streaming service is a different product.

Before release, follow the pre-order control offered by your storefront. After release, the same official product page should be your starting point for installation. Do not interpret a pre-order confirmation as an immediate download or as a purchase of the full-game unlock.

Apple explains how to view and cancel pre-orders in its [pre-order support guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118414). If you want to change your plans, manage the order through your Apple account rather than giving payment details to a third-party “early access” page.

For a household device, decide which account will make the purchase before someone authorizes it. Keep the receipt associated with that account. That is ordinary purchase housekeeping; it does not establish Family Sharing, cross-buy or access on another platform for this particular game.

## Before buying, check these three separate decisions

**First, check installation eligibility.** The model name, installed operating system and device's own App Store compatibility result all matter. Do not infer eligibility from the age of the phone alone. Our [compatibility article](/no-mans-sky-iphone-compatibility/) explains why the public listing's older-device entries need careful interpretation.

**Second, check your essential feature.** A solo explorer and someone planning to join friends are making different purchases. The port's platform-specific restrictions are covered in the [mobile comparison](/no-mans-sky-mobile-vs-pc-console/). A recognizable title does not guarantee identical functionality across editions.

**Third, check the actual purchase offer.** Read the local price and the wording of the unlock. Owning the game on Steam, a console or another Apple platform is not, by itself, evidence that this mobile purchase has already been paid for. Confirm entitlements through the relevant official store instead of assuming a shared title means a shared licence.

## Make the free opening section a useful check

When the public release arrives, try a small set of ordinary actions before deciding on the unlock. Move around, read inventory text, interact with an object and inspect the controls you intend to use. These are suggested checks, not results from a mobile test we have performed.

If you already have a compatible controller, compare your comfort with touch and controller input. There is no need to buy an accessory before seeing whether you enjoy the game. Apple's [wireless controller instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111099) cover pairing; button and feature support can still vary by app.

For example, a player intending to use a phone on the bus might value readable menus and easy interruptions more than a large-screen setup at home. Someone buying the iPad edition mainly for an existing desktop save should resolve save-transfer support before treating the mobile unlock as a companion purchase. Your intended routine should drive the decision.

## Is there an Android release or APK?

The launch announcement and product page cited here concern iPhone and iPad. They do not establish an Android release date or an official Android installation package. That is a limit of the confirmed announcement, not a prediction that Android will never receive a port.

An iOS pre-order cannot be installed as an Android APK. If you encounter a download claiming otherwise, check for an explicit link from Hello Games before treating it as an official release. The safe next step for an Android-only player is to follow the developer's platform announcements, rather than pay for supposed access to this Apple launch.
