*Discover some of the brightest apps to help you reach your NY resolutions in 2025*:

## Apps to break BAD HABITS

Breaking a bad habit is a great starting point for a better you in 2025. It won't be easy, but if you're determined, make an effort, and keep your faith up, YOU CAN DO IT! And you can get extra help from top-rated apps like **[Quitzilla](https://www.quitzilla.com/)**, **['Days Since'](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/days-since-quit-habit-tracker/id1445348921)** (iPhone and Apple watch), or **'Bad Habit Break'** ([Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shpavda.bad_habit_break)/ [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bad-habit-break/id1658588991)).

## Best MOTIVATION apps

There are many motivation apps out there, as having the inner drive and perseverance to achieve anything in life, is the essence of success.

One of the best motivation apps is the **[Loop Habit Tracker](https://loophabits.org/)** (Android only), as it doesn't have any ads, it's 100% free and open source. It's also pretty simple, easy to use, and it features detailed stats of your progress over time.

[**'Motivation - Daily Quotes'**](https://motivation.app/) is another inciting application, that can genuinely inspire and empower you, or just give you the kick you need to get something done! *"A cloudy day can never bother you if you bring your own sunshine"*.

If you get bored easily, you'll love the [**Habitica**](https://habitica.com/static/home) productivity app, as it motivates you by **turning every goal you set into a game**! How awesome is that?

## Apps to help you plan and excel in 2025

Clear planning is crucial to progress, especially in today's busy world. Even more, if you set big goals for yourself, as they are achieved one step at a time.

The [**Todoist**](https://todoist.com/) is one of the most comprehensive **task managements** apps you'd find, designed for handling both work and personal chores with ease.

Another brilliant app, that would give you a totally different perspective on planning and productivity, and help you reach your NY resolutions, is **[Co-Star](https://www.costarastrology.com/)**. It provides **personalized advice** on being the best you can be, based on your specific, date, time and place of birth! That's right, it's an astrology app, BUT even if you're skeptical, you'll be amazed how scarily accurate it is! The tricky part is, you need to know the exact time of day you were born, in order to trigger a detailed star map of the sky the moment you took your first breath...

And last, but not least, good planning requires a **clear mind** as well. So, whether you're familiar with mindfulness and meditation, or not, do give the **[Headspace app](https://www.headspace.com/)** a go in 2025. You won't regret it once you reap the benefits of a calmer, more focused, and happier you!
