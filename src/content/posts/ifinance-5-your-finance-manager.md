## Quick verdict

iFinance 5 is best understood as a detailed personal-finance manager for people already invested in Apple devices. It can organise accounts, budgets, categories and reports, but its value depends heavily on whether bank connections work reliably in your country and whether you prefer local control over an account-agnostic mobile service.

## What the app does in 2026

The app is designed to consolidate income, spending and account balances, with manual entry and supported transaction imports. Users can create budgets, scheduled transactions and reports, then sync data across compatible Apple devices. Its desktop-oriented depth is an advantage for careful household bookkeeping, though it can feel heavier than a simple expense tracker.

## Main features

- Multiple accounts, categories and transaction types
- Budgets and recurring or scheduled transactions
- Charts and reports for income, spending and balances
- Import and supported online-banking connections
- Apple-device synchronization and backups

## Pricing and fees

Pricing and purchase options can differ between macOS and mobile storefronts and may change with upgrades. Check the developer’s current store listing for the exact licence, subscription or in-app purchase offered in your region. Do not rely on the price quoted in the original 2023 article.

## Limitations and risks

No finance app can guarantee that every bank feed will stay available. Connection coverage, authentication rules and imported categories vary by institution and country. Users should maintain backups, reconcile balances and avoid treating automated categorisation as an audited financial record.

## Who should use it?

Best for Apple users who want more structure than a minimalist spending tracker and are willing to maintain their data. Before paying, verify support for your devices, bank, currency and preferred sync method.

## App-Tipps recommendation

Check the live price, permissions and account terms on your own device before subscribing or moving money. Start with the free tier or shortest commitment where one is available, and keep screenshots or receipts for paid transactions.
