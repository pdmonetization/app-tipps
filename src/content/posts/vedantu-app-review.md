Now is a great time to learn something new or widen our knowledge of various subjects. The lockdown we are all experiencing might be harder for children at schools. Like all services, schools are closed, too, meaning that the vast majority of children are not able to receive an education. While some schools are offering online classes, not all of them have the same capacity to do so. The Vedantu app is an excellent solution for digital learning for school kids, especially at times like this.

## What is the Vedantu app?

Vedantu is one of the best online learning platforms in India. In fact, it was a user choice in the best app awards 2019 in the category of essentials by Google. Initially, it focused on school kids and offered lessons in different subjects for the classes from 1 to 12, but now it covers a wide spread of topics, including coding.

To get an idea of the studies that the Vedantu app provides, here are some subjects well-covered:

- Maths
- English
- EVS
- Science & SST
- PCMB

That's not all; it also offers co-curricular courses for different grades. Every child can find something interesting for themselves. Thay they can start learning and mastering during this lockdown and after it. The Vedantu app offers classes in coding, photography, grammar, turbo maths, and many other exciting and interesting subjects.

## Why Vedantu?

The app has in-built technology for the platform WAVE that enables the live interactive learning experience. It makes learning with the Vedantu app more accessible and more fun. The app offers LIVE in-class unlimited doubt solving to make the lessons even more dynamic. It also ensures that students have a compelling concept of the learning experience.

LIVE-in classes that the Vedantu app offers also have live quizzes. They are gamified, which makes them more engaging. During the examinations, students can see the leaderboard, which encourages them to outdo themselves and get better results.

Last but not least, the app has a hotspot feature. With the help of it, the app captures students' responses in real time. Enabling teachers to see how well the students understand the lessons.

## Features of the Vedantu app

The features of the app that make it an incredible source of education do not stop here. Let's discuss what you can do with it.

- **Attend live video lectures for classes 1-12**

As explained, with the Vudantu app, you can attend various video lectures for classes 1-12. You will have access to lessons from the best teachers in India. There are many lessons specifically for you to help you prepare for your exams. You can prepare for IT JEE, JEE, both principal and advanced, KVPY, NEET, NTSE, olympiad, or boards exam.

- **Get access to the complete study package**

Vedantu offers live videos, but with it comes the complete study package to help you learn. The study package includes syllabuses for each of your classes, homework help, and even sample papers! You will have everything you need to learn effectively.

- **Take part in all India mock tests for free**

Use the app to prepare for the exams. The most important part of passing exams is practicing. You can do it on the Vedantu app too! The app offers multiple mock tests for various abovementioned tests to help you be ready for the actual tests.

Vedantu understands the importance of affordable education. Especially when times are this hard and not many children have access to the quality education provided by schools. It is why you can now access the premium content, too, for free! Download the Vedantu app from Google Play or App Store today and never stop learning!
