> **Updated and fact-checked:** 14 August 2026. Features, prices and availability can vary by country, account and platform.

## Quick verdict

Vedantu offers a broad Indian online-learning platform rather than one simple app subscription. It can suit students who need structured live teaching and exam preparation, but course quality, schedule and price must be judged at the batch level.

## What it offers in 2026

The platform advertises courses for school grades, CBSE and ICSE-aligned subjects, and competitive exams including JEE and NEET. Depending on the programme, students may receive live classes, recordings, tests, notes, question practice and doubt support.

## Main features

- Live online classes and recorded revision
- School and competitive-exam programmes
- Tests and performance analysis
- Digital study materials and teacher notes
- Doubt-solving and mentorship on selected plans

## Pricing and fees

There is no single Vedantu price. Fees range widely by grade, subject, duration, class size and promotion, and some pages show EMI-style monthly figures rather than the total. Get the full written price, schedule, refund terms and included materials before paying.

## Limitations and risks

Marketing outcomes do not guarantee marks or admission. Attend a demo where available, verify the teacher and language, and check whether classes fit school hours. Parents should supervise account, payment and chat use for younger learners.

## Who is it best for?

Best for students who benefit from scheduled instruction and will use the tests and support. Self-directed learners may find recorded or lower-cost resources sufficient.

## Our practical recommendation

Check the live checkout, permissions and cancellation terms on your own device. Use the free tier, trial or shortest plan first where possible, and keep transaction records for paid services.
