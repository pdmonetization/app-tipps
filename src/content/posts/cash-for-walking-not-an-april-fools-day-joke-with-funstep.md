FunStep is a free mobile application, that rewards you with money for staying active and healthy! No catch, just CASH... It's indeed as good as it sounds 😇

## How does the FunStep app work?

1. Download the FunStep app on an [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.funstep.run) phone or an [iPhone](https://y2viyzjhm2.qttsudi.com/0d3jrx?819e187=420065e8f577f3f3e1a89988e72dc3b4).
2. Create your personalized FunStep account.
3. Start using the application as a step counter.
4. Earn 'FUNSTEP Coins' - the more you walk, the more you earn!
5. Spend your in-app coins on products and services, or convert them into crypto currency.

It's that simple and that awesome! No wonder giants like **Nike** and **Adidas** have chosen to collaborate with the activity app. Furthermore, FunStep partners with **Google Fitbit** too, making it fully compatible with their wearable gadgets!
