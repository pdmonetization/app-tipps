This **Palworld Online FAQ** covers what Garena has confirmed—and what remains unknown—about the upcoming mobile MMORPG. The short version is that it is an officially licensed 2026 game for mobile, but it does not yet have an exact release date, public store listing, confirmed price or published device requirements.

The distinction between confirmed and assumed information matters. Palworld Online is attracting searches before launch, which also attracts placeholder dates, unofficial APKs and claims borrowed from the original Palworld or the separate Palworld Mobile project.

## Is Palworld Online a real official game?

Yes. Garena announced Palworld Online on August 3, 2026 as a multiplayer survival adventure based on the Palworld intellectual property. Pocketpair granted the license, and Pocketpair CEO Takuro Mizobe provided a statement supporting the collaboration.

Garena is the developer and publisher. Pocketpair is not building this version directly, which means launch, service and account questions should be answered by Garena rather than inferred from the original game's policies.

Officially licensed does not mean identical. The game has a new setting, original story material and an MMO structure created for mobile.

## When is the Palworld Online release date?

The only official release window is 2026. Garena has not announced a month, day, launch time or regional sequence.

A retailer placeholder, search-result date or fan countdown is not a confirmation. Mobile games can move through closed tests, regional soft launches and staged storefront releases before worldwide availability. Garena has not yet said whether Palworld Online will use any of those stages.

Our dedicated [Palworld Online release-date tracker](/palworld-online-release-date/) lists the announced features and missing launch details without converting estimates into facts.

## Is Palworld Online available for pre-registration?

There is no official App Store or Google Play registration listing linked from Garena's announcement at the time of writing. Garena instead directs players to official social and community channels for future updates.

When registration begins, use a first-party route:

1. Open Garena's website or a verified Palworld Online account.
2. Follow its store link rather than searching for an APK.
3. Confirm the developer or publisher name on the listing.
4. Review the data and purchase disclosures before installing.
5. Ignore sites requiring payment to "reserve" a place.

Pre-registration rewards, beta access and launch bonuses have not been announced. Any specific reward list currently circulating is unofficial unless Garena publishes it.

## Will Palworld Online be free to play?

Garena has not announced the price or monetization model. It could be free-to-play, premium or use another structure, but there is no basis for selecting one as fact.

The official announcement does not mention:

- An upfront purchase price
- In-app purchases
- Randomized character or Pal acquisition
- Battle passes
- Subscriptions
- Energy or stamina systems
- Advertising
- Cosmetic stores

Garena operates successful free-to-play services, but company history is not a product specification. The correct time to judge value is after store disclosures and a playable build reveal how progression interacts with spending.

## Is Palworld Online coming to Android and iOS?

Garena describes the project as mobile-first, and current launch reporting identifies Android and iOS. Official storefront URLs and minimum operating-system versions are not yet public.

There is no confirmed PC, Mac, PlayStation, Xbox or Nintendo version of Palworld Online. Those platforms already have access to versions of Pocketpair's original game, while the new spin-off is designed around touchscreen accessibility.

An Android release does not automatically mean Garena will distribute a separate APK. Wait for the official installation method.

## What phone specifications will Palworld Online require?

Minimum and recommended specifications have not been published. Garena says the game is being optimized across a wide range of phone specifications, but that statement does not identify supported chipsets, RAM, storage or frame-rate targets.

Do not use the original PC requirements to estimate a phone requirement. Palworld Online is a different build with different assets, networking and world management.

The useful specifications to wait for are:

1. Minimum Android and iOS versions
2. Supported processor or device generations
3. Minimum RAM
4. Initial download size
5. Additional data downloaded after installation
6. Recommended free storage
7. 30 and 60 frames-per-second targets
8. Graphics-quality options
9. Controller support

A device can technically install a game and still deliver an unpleasant experience, so recommended requirements will matter more than the bare minimum.

## Can Palworld Online be played offline?

No offline mode has been announced. The game is described as an MMO with shared settlements, meetings between players, cooperative encounters and PvP, all of which imply a continuing server connection.

Some MMOs allow limited solo activities, but solo and offline are not the same. A player may be able to explore or complete story objectives alone while still remaining connected to Garena's servers.

If offline play is essential, Pocketpair's original Palworld is the safer option. Our [Palworld Online vs Palworld comparison](/palworld-online-vs-palworld/) explains the difference between the released sandbox and Garena's persistent mobile world.

## Does Palworld Online have multiplayer, PvE and PvP?

Yes. Garena has confirmed group formation, shared settlements, cooperative PvE encounters, open-world bosses, rare-Pal hunts and competitive PvP matches.

The announcement does not reveal:

- Maximum party and guild sizes
- Server population
- Ranked or casual PvP formats
- Whether open-world PvP is optional
- Matchmaking rules
- Raid sizes
- Cross-region play
- Voice-chat support

Players concerned about forced PvP should wait for a clear server or mode explanation. The presence of PvP does not prove that every player can be attacked in the open world.

## Can you capture, breed and build with Pals?

Capturing and raising Pals are confirmed. Pals can accompany exploration, help automate production and defend collaborative settlements. Garena also references hunting rare Pals with other players.

Breeding has not been described as clearly in the English Palworld Online announcement, so the exact inheritance and breeding systems should not be assumed from the original game. Shared ownership also raises questions: it is unknown whether settlement members can use one another's workers, move deployed Pals or access common storage.

These rules will strongly affect group play and deserve an official explanation before launch.

## Is Palworld Online the same as Palworld Mobile?

No. Palworld Online is Garena's mobile MMO. Palworld Mobile is a separate licensed project from PUBG Studios and Krafton.

They have different official sites, developers and public design language. Palworld Online emphasizes a persistent shared ecosystem and original narrative. Palworld Mobile currently emphasizes survival, direct combat, crafting and multiplayer in a presentation closer to the original loop.

See [Palworld Online vs Palworld Mobile](/palworld-online-vs-palworld-mobile/) for a full side-by-side explanation.

## Will Palworld accounts, saves or purchases transfer?

No transfer system has been announced between Palworld Online and any other Palworld game. Assume a new account, character and collection until Garena says otherwise.

The following are not confirmed to carry over:

- Original Palworld save files
- Steam, Xbox, PlayStation or Mac characters
- Captured Pals
- Bases and resources
- Achievements
- Mods
- Purchased copies or downloadable content
- Palworld Mobile progress

Similar branding does not make save formats or payment systems compatible.

## Will Palworld Online support controllers?

Controller support has not been announced. Garena is emphasizing a streamlined touch interface and optional one-tap controls for building, relocation and Pal deployment.

Those features suggest that the game should be fully usable without a controller. They do not rule out Bluetooth controller support. Players who require physical controls should wait for an official compatibility list instead of assuming that accessories supported by other Garena games will work.

## How can you follow Palworld Online safely?

Use Garena's announcement as the starting point. It links the official Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Discord and Reddit communities. A genuine registration or beta campaign should be repeated through those channels.

Avoid search ads and download pages that:

- Offer an APK before Garena announces one
- Promise guaranteed closed-beta access for payment
- Request an existing Steam or console password
- Claim transferable premium Pals
- Use a slightly misspelled Garena or Palworld domain
- Install a separate "launcher verification" app

The project is real, but that does not make every page using its name legitimate.
