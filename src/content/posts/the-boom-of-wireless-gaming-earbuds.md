## What are gaming earbuds?

In-ear headphones are typically wireless and with **minimal latency** to avoid game disruption. They should provide for a better **sound quality**, as well as **noise isolation**. A decent **mic** is also a must.

Gaming earbuds are usually more expensive than other in-ears, but they are **multifunctional**, and can be used for making calls and listening to music. So, if you're a gamer, and especially if you're keen on mobile gaming, most models are well worth the extra money!

## Are AirPods good gaming headphones?

AirPods are considered good for gaming, especially when paired with an iPhone or another Apple device. Naturally, the latest models, like **[AirPods 4](https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-airpods/airpods-4) with Active Noise Cancellation**, are better in terms of lagging and mobile gaming compatibility. ## The advantages of gaming earbuds over full-size headphones

🎧

In-ear headphones are, generally, better for mobile gaming, as they are **lightweight**, causing less discomfort during extended gameplay, as well as in hot weather. People wearing glasses and hats also find them more comfortable. Plus, their **pocket size** makes them easier to carry outside.

Last but not least, gaming in-ears tend to be much **cheaper** than over-ears!
