Just now, PUBG Mobile announced one of the biggest gaming partnerships ever. This time, the battle royale game is working with Maserati, an Italian company that makes high-end cars. As of right now, we are still determining what this collaboration will bring to players because we've only seen teasers for it.

Through the post, we can see that two cars from the manufacturer have been promoted, and it's likely that we'll see them in the game soon. The first car is the Maserati Levante, which is their best SUV. The second car is the Maserati Cielo, which is their newest spyder. I'm sure PUBG Mobile players can't wait to get behind the wheel of these cars and trucks.

This is not the first time a luxury car company has worked with the game. They have previously worked with Koenigsegg, McLaren, Tesla, Lamborghini, and Dodge. Now, they want to add another to their list, so they are working with Maserati. We have yet to determine what this collaboration will be about, but we know it will be exciting.

PUBG Mobile is one of the most popular mobile games, and it has stayed at the top by giving players new things to try all the time. Their most recent project is with Lionel Messi, the world's most famous football player. In the coming weeks, there will be special PUBG x Messi events that players can check out.
