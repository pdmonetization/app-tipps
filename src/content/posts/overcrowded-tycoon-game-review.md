Overcrowded Tycoon is an incredibly addictive, park-management game that will keep you hooked for hours. In it, you'll have to build your theme park and make sure all of your visitors are happy, lest they leave and take their money with them! It's easy to get started – just drag and drop various attractions onto the map to create paths to guide visitors around your park. Guarantee visitors will stop at as many attractions as possible before proceeding to the exit at the opposite end of the map.

## **Overcrowded Tycoon: Gameplay & Tips**

If you're a fan of simulation games or tycoon titles, then Overcrowded Tycoon is an excellent choice. The gameplay is smooth and flawless and will hook you from level one. It's surprisingly deep, with a ton of content to keep you engaged and new challenges to keep things fresh. In addition, the game boasts gorgeous, downright charming graphics, making it even more appealing. Overcrowded Tycoon is worth every penny and earns our editor's choice award with flying colors.

If you're looking for a quality game to waste away some time on your phone or tablet, look no further than Overcrowded Tycoon; it's all kinds of awesome! It is one fun of a time-killing simulation game that will let you dive into gameplay where you can:

### **Create a Beautiful Landscape & Decorate with Wonders**

It's easy to get sidetracked when it comes to building your park. Make sure you add functional and pleasing items to your visitors' eyes. Our favorite touches include waterfalls, colorful flowers, fountains, and statues. Visitors enjoy getting a break from walking around in natural settings by strolling through themed areas like a tropical greenhouse or even a petting zoo with playground equipment. Plan out your park ahead of time to know where you want to place each amenity before you drop them into place. It might be fun to wing it sometimes, but if your game plan is too loose, you'll waste valuable resources on things that aren't worth investing in.

### **Build Upgrades & Unlock Items**

When it comes to building your park, Overcrowded Tycoon is a bit of a puzzler. However, some basic rules should help you ensure your playground is ready for visitors. First, take advantage of all available upgrades – don't leave any money on the table if you can avoid it. Once that's done, consider focusing on unlocking items that will let you complete tasks more efficiently (this will save you time and money). You'll have everything at your disposal in no time – and sooner rather than later! Finally, expand your park regularly by purchasing additional land and upgrading existing attractions. With these tips in mind, you're guaranteed to succeed with ease. So, what are you waiting for? It's time to get started!

### **Manage Attractions Efficiently**

The best way to keep visitors happy is to make sure they're taken care of. Build restaurants and shops around your park and ensure you've got everything they need. Be efficient in your design, as new shops require much attention to keep them running smoothly. You can also build attractions, like roller coasters and trampolines, that will be popular among guests and provide a good return on investment. But don't build too many at once, or you won't have enough resources to maintain them all.

### **Unlock new features**

Park upgrade options are unlocked by earning three-star ratings in levels. Earning a three-star rating unlocks different building types, each with a unique function and appearance. The maximum park rating is five stars; it's impossible to achieve all of them in one game. Do you have what it takes to earn five stars on every level?

### **Practice makes perfect**

The more you play Overcrowded Tycoon, the better you'll get at managing your park. And you'll want to put some time in—this game is incredibly addictive! Playing unlocks many new items and upgrades, so there are always new ways to improve. Even if you don't win a level, it doesn't mean it was a waste of time (or energy). Plus, your visitors won't get mad if they see their favorite attractions regularly. They just like to see something different every once in a while. So make sure you have a variety of rides and amenities on hand. Also, keep an eye on how many people can fit into each attraction at one time (you can find that information by tapping on any ride or facility). Too many people waiting around for rides will lower your overall visitor happiness rating!

### **Use helpful hints wisely**

They take time away from your overall playtime. As always, there's no penalty for failure! Play as many times as you want until you get those elusive five-star ratings! If you're stumped about how to complete a task or build something, tap on Tips on any level menu screen. You can also view tips by tapping on question marks that appear when playing levels or during tutorials. And if that's still not enough help, there are always friendly video walkthroughs on YouTube.
